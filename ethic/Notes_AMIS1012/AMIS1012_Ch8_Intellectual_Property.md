## 1. Introduction to IP（p4–5）

- **Intellectual property (IP)** = **creations of the mind**：inventions（发明）、literary and artistic works（文学艺术作品）、以及商业上使用的 symbols, names, images, designs（符号、名称、图像、设计）。
- 做生意必须了解怎样**使用、保护、维护**自己拥有的 IP 权利。
- 公司的责任：**从 IP 获取收入**并**阻止他人这样做**。
- IP 是**无形资产 (intangible asset)**，但可能比公司的**实体资产更值钱**；因为能带来**战略优势 (strategic advantage)**，公司会严密保护。

## 2. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)（p6–7）

- **IPR** = 从**人类智力活动成果**产生、具**经济利益**的权利；由人脑思考产生对人有用的产品或流程而来的权利（Abduh & Fajaruddin, 2021）。
- 最常见的 IPR：**copyrights, patents, trade secrets, trademarks**。
- IPR 让拥有者**完全受惠**于自己从想法变成现实的作品；
- 让他**阻止他人未经许可使用、玩弄或干涉**其财产；
- 他有**法律权利起诉**侵权者，要求**停止侵权并赔偿损失**。

**IPR 为什么重要（综合 p5–7，考题最常问）**：

| Reason | 说明 |
|---|---|
| **Exclusive right to commercialise / earn income** | 只有拥有者能从作品赚钱（销售、授权） |
| **Prevent others from copying / using without permission** | 阻止抄袭、仿冒、盗版 |
| **Legal remedy** | 可起诉，要求停止与赔偿 |
| **Competitive / strategic advantage** | 独有的技术或品牌让对手无法复制 → 市场地位 |
| **Valuable intangible asset** | 可授权、出售、吸引投资，可能比实体资产更值钱 |
| **Encourage innovation & creativity** | 创作者得到回报，愿意继续投入研发与创作 |
| **Protect reputation / consumers** | 防止劣质仿冒品损害品牌、误导消费者 |

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *Intellectual property rights give the owner the exclusive legal right to use and commercially benefit from creations of the mind, to prevent others from using them without permission, and to sue infringers for an injunction and compensation. They turn intangible ideas into valuable assets that provide a competitive advantage.*

### ✅ 满分答法 — May 2025 Q4a: Examine why IPR are important to Nintendo. Provide relevant examples. (5 marks)
1. **Exclusive commercial benefit** – IPR give Nintendo the exclusive right to earn income from its games, consoles and characters; *e.g.* only Nintendo (or its licensees) can sell Super Mario games and merchandise. (1–2)
2. **Preventing unauthorised use** – Nintendo can stop others from using its name, logos and characters without permission; *e.g.* a company selling unlicensed Pokémon plush toys can be stopped. (1)
3. **Legal remedy** – if its IP is infringed, Nintendo can sue for an injunction and damages; *e.g.* taking action against websites distributing pirated Nintendo Switch games. (1)
4. **Competitive advantage and brand value** – its characters and brand are intangible assets worth more than physical assets; protecting them keeps competitors from copying and maintains customer trust in the quality of genuine Nintendo products. (1)

### ✅ 满分答法 — Oct 2024 Q4a: Examine why IPR are important to Domitri. (5 marks)
1. His encryption algorithm is his proprietary creation; IPR give him the **exclusive right to commercialise** it and earn income, e.g. through licence fees from technology companies. (1–2)
2. IPR give him the **authority to permit or restrict** its use — a software company must obtain his permission before integrating it. (1)
3. IPR give him **legal protection**: if someone uses the algorithm without permission, he can sue to stop them and claim compensation. (1)
4. Because the algorithm is **widely adopted**, it is a highly valuable intangible asset and a **competitive advantage**; protection prevents competitors from copying it and encourages him to keep innovating. (1)

### ✅ 满分答法 — Jan 2025 Q4a: Determine the importance of protecting IPR for Cadiz. Discuss how IPR contributes to competitive advantage. (5 marks)
**Importance (2–3):** Cadiz's AI-powered portal, unique design features and branding are intangible assets created through significant investment. Without IPR, the competitor could keep using them freely, as happened after launch. IPR give Cadiz the exclusive right to use and license these assets and the legal right to stop the competitor and claim compensation.
**Competitive advantage (2–3):** IPR make Cadiz's innovations exclusive — competitors cannot copy the AI features or look-alike branding, so customers associate the unique experience only with Cadiz. This differentiates Cadiz in the market, protects its market share and brand reputation, allows it to earn licensing income, and makes the company more attractive to investors.

### ✅ 满分答法 — May 2026 Q4a: Explain TWO reasons why IPR are important for the game creators. (6 marks)
1. **Exclusive right to profit from their work** – the original characters, music, logo and gameplay are the developers' creations; IPR give them the exclusive right to sell, license and earn income from the game and merchandise, rewarding their investment and encouraging further creativity. Now that the game is viral, this income is at stake. (3)
2. **Legal power to stop copying and protect their brand** – IPR let the developers prevent competitors who copy the design and branding from using them, and sue for an injunction and damages; this protects their market share and stops players being confused by imitations that could damage the game's reputation. (3)

## 3. Types of IP（p8–27）⭐

### 3.0 先看决策表：保护什么 → 用哪种 IP（slide 没有，考试最有用）

| 要保护的东西 | 最适合的 IP | 为什么 | PixelPanda 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **新的发明、技术方法、独特功能/流程** | **Patent** | 保护**想法/功能本身**，即使别人独立写出不同的 code 也不能用 | 用相机扫描食物解锁关卡的**技术方法**（若符合 novelty、inventive step） |
| **品牌名、logo、slogan、角色名**（用来区分商品来源） | **Trademark** | 防止别人用相同/相似标志造成混淆；可无限续期 | "Kuih Quest" 名称与熊猫 logo |
| **原创作品的具体表达**：code、美术、角色设计、音乐、文字、影片、网页设计 | **Copyright** | 创作完成即**自动**产生；保护表达，不保护想法 | 游戏原始码、Onde 的角色美术、原创音乐 |
| **保密的商业资讯**：原始码（未公开）、演算法参数、配方、客户名单、策略 | **Trade secret** | 只要**保密**就一直受保护；不能注册 | 游戏引擎的未公开原始码、玩家数据分析方法 |

> 判断口诀：**做法 → patent；名字 → trademark；作品 → copyright；秘密 → trade secret。**

### 3.1 Patents（p9–10）

- **Patent** = 给予发明人的**专利权**，让他在**一段固定时间内独占**把发明商业化，排除他人。
- 任何**新而有益**的东西叫 "innovation"。
- **马来西亚**：由 **Patents Act 1983** 与 **Patents Regulations 1986** 规范。
- 可直接在马来西亚申请，注册后**全马有效**。
- **可专利的三个条件**：**new (novelty)**、**involves an inventive step**、**industrially applicable**。
- 权利：**实施 (exploit)** 专利发明、**转让或移转 (assign/transmit)** 专利、**签订授权合约 (licence contracts)**。
- ⚠️ **期限**：slide 写 "expire **15 years after the date of grant**"——这是**旧条文**。*Extra*：Patents Act 1983 修订后，现行规定是**自申请日起 20 年**（需缴年费维持）。考试若照 slide 写 15 年，建议加一句 "(currently 20 years from the filing date)"。
- *Extra*：专利申请要**公开**发明内容；**数学方法、科学理论、纯演算法本身**一般**不能**获得专利，但把它应用在**具体的技术方案**（例如一个加密系统/装置）则可能可以。

### ✅ 满分答法 — Tutorial 8 Q3: What is a patent? TWO laws governing patents in Malaysia.
A patent is an exclusive right granted to an inventor for a new, inventive and industrially applicable invention, allowing the owner to commercially exploit it for a fixed period while excluding others. In Malaysia it is governed by the **Patents Act 1983** and the **Patents Regulations 1986**.

### 3.2 Trademarks（p11–16）

- **Trademark** = 能够**区分一家企业与其他企业的商品或服务**的**标志 (sign)**；受 IPR 保护。
- **马来西亚**：**Trademarks Act 2019**——规范商品与服务商标的注册，并落实相关条约。
- *Extra*：马来西亚注册商标有效 **10 年，可无限续期**。
- 注册前必须**彻底调查**（避免与已有商标冲突）。

**五个 spectrum（依强度/显著性 distinctiveness，由弱到强）（p12–15）**：

```
  WEAK <────────────────────────────────────────────────────────> STRONG
  not distinctive,           acquired distinctiveness,          distinctive,
  cannot be protected        maybe protected                    can be protected

   GENERIC     DESCRIPTIVE     SUGGESTIVE      ARBITRARY      FANCIFUL/COINED
   Clock       Cold and        Sweetarts       BlackBerry     Kodak
               Creamy (ice                     Apple          Exxon
               cream)                          (computers)    Adidas
```
*看图重点：越往右越**容易注册与执行**；越往左越难（generic 几乎不可能）。*

| Spectrum | 定义 | Slide 例子 | 强度 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Generic** | 与商品/服务**直接相关的普通名称**；**极难或不可能**注册与执行 | **CLOCK**（卖时钟） | 最弱 |
| **Descriptive** | **直接描述**商品/服务的性质 | **COLD AND CREAMY**（冰淇淋） | 弱（除非取得 secondary meaning） |
| **Suggestive** | **暗示**某种特质，但**不直接描述** | **SWEETARTS**（糖果） | 中 |
| **Arbitrary** | 有**普通意义**，但用在**无关**的商品/服务 | **BLACKBERRY**（手机）、Apple（电脑） | 强 |
| **Fanciful / Coined** | **除了作为商标外没有意义**（自创字）；**最容易注册与执行** | **KODAK**、Exxon | 最强 |

- p14 的另一张图把 ASPIRIN、XEROX 放在 generic——意思是它们原本是强商标，但因为被大众当成**通用名词**而变弱（*extra*：这叫 **genericide**）。
- p16：新闻 "Trademark, copyright infringement still occur despite wider awareness of IP rights"（NST 2024）→ 说明马来西亚侵权仍普遍。

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *Trademarks fall on a spectrum of distinctiveness: generic (the common name of the product, e.g. "Clock" — almost impossible to register), descriptive (directly describes the product, e.g. "Cold and Creamy" ice cream), suggestive (hints at a quality, e.g. "Sweetarts"), arbitrary (a common word used for an unrelated product, e.g. "BlackBerry" phones) and fanciful/coined (an invented word with no other meaning, e.g. "Kodak" — easiest to register and enforce).*

### ✅ 满分答法 — Oct 2024 Q4c: Domitri wants to register a trademark for his brand name. Explain the FIVE spectrums with a relevant example for each. (10 marks)
2 分一个（定义 + 例子，最好用他的加密软件品牌来举例）：
1. **Generic** – the common name for the product or service itself; it is extremely difficult or impossible to register and enforce. *e.g.* naming his product "**Encryption Software**" (slide example: CLOCK for clocks).
2. **Descriptive** – directly describes the nature or feature of the product; weak and usually registrable only after it acquires distinctiveness. *e.g.* "**RealTime Encrypt**" (slide: COLD AND CREAMY ice cream).
3. **Suggestive** – hints at a quality of the product without directly describing it; the customer needs some imagination. *e.g.* "**LockStream**" suggesting secure data flow (slide: SWEETARTS).
4. **Arbitrary** – a common word with an ordinary meaning applied to an unrelated product; strong protection. *e.g.* "**Durian**" for encryption software (slide: BLACKBERRY phones).
5. **Fanciful / coined** – an invented word with no meaning other than as a trademark; the strongest and easiest to register and enforce. *e.g.* "**Crytavo**" (slide: KODAK). Domitri should choose a fanciful or arbitrary name for the strongest protection.

### 3.3 Copyrights and related rights（p17–22）

- **Copyright** = 作者、艺术家或创作者对其**已以实体形式表达 (expressed physically)** 的**原创作品**进行**商业利用**的法律权利；也包括**阻止他人未经同意复制或重用**的权利。
- **马来西亚 Copyright Act 1987**：主要的版权法，规定创作者与版权持有人的权利与义务，包括侵权的补救方法；依据国际标准起草，包括 **Berne Convention**（保护文学与艺术作品）。
- **Copyright (Amendment) Act 2012 (Act A1420)**：综合版包含到 2012 年的修订。
- **Copyright (Amendment) Act 2022**：符合 **RCEP、Marrakesh Treaty、CPTPP** 的国际标准；加强执法，特别是**数位环境**——新增涉及**串流技术 (streaming technology)** 的罪行（针对非法串流装置/服务）。

**期限（p21）**：

| 地区 | Literary, musical, artistic works |
|---|---|
| Singapore, US, EU | **Life of the author + 70 years** |
| **Malaysia** | **Life of the creator + 50 years** after death（遗产继承人再受惠 50 年） |
| 期满后 | 进入 **public domain**（公共领域） |

*Extra*：copyright **不需要注册**，作品一被创作并以实体形式记录就自动受保护；它保护**表达 (expression)**，**不保护想法 (idea)**——别人可以做"扫描食物解锁关卡"的游戏，只要不抄你的 code 和美术（想法要靠 patent）。

**Copyright 的好处（p22）**：
1. **鼓励想象与创新**：给作者、艺术家、制作人赞誉与**公平的金钱回报**，提升产量与作品品质。
2. **让企业更有效地投入**作品的**生产、成长与全球发行** → 改善全世界的文化、资讯、娱乐享受，促进经济与社会发展。

### 3.4 Trade secrets（p23–27）

- **Trade secrets** = 对**机密资讯**的 IPR，可**授权或出售**。任何让企业有**竞争优势**且**未向第三方披露**的机密资讯都可受保护。
- 包括**技术资讯**（研究与工程资料、工程图、流程表、**电脑软件原始码**）与**商业资讯**（营销策略、生产资料、商业活动记录）。
- **马来西亚**：'trade secrets' 与 **'confidential information'** 可互换，法律正式承认的是后者。
- **不能注册**；但被他人利用时可以**采取法律行动**。
- 间接保护的成文法：**Competition Act 2010**（禁止反竞争行为，某些情况下包括滥用商业秘密）；**Penal Code**（**criminal breach of trust** 适用于滥用机密资讯）。
- 马来西亚**没有专门的 trade secret 法**，但 **contract law、common law、statutory law** 提供有效保护。

**保护方法（p27）**：

| Internal measures | External measure |
|---|---|
| **Strengthen employee agreements**（合约中的保密条款、离职后的 non-compete/non-solicit） | **Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)** 与合作伙伴、供应商、投资人签 |
| **Implement access controls**（只让需要的人看；加密；记录存取） | |
| **Address everyday practices**（文件标示 "Confidential"、清桌政策、员工训练、离职时收回资料） | |

### ✅ 满分答法 — Tutorial 8 Q7: Explain TWO methods to protect trade secrets.
1. **Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)** – before sharing confidential information with employees, partners, suppliers or investors, the company requires them to sign an NDA that legally obliges them to keep it secret, giving grounds for legal action if they disclose it.
2. **Access controls** – restrict access to trade secrets (e.g. source code, formulas) to only those employees who need it, using passwords, encryption, access logs and physical security, so that the secret is less likely to leak and misuse can be traced.
（Also: strengthen employee agreements with confidentiality clauses; address everyday practices such as labelling and training.）

### ✅ 满分答法 — May 2025 Q4b: Suggest TWO most suitable types of IPR Nintendo should consider for their market content, with justification. (10 marks)
5 分一个（name 1 + what it protects 1–2 + why suitable for Nintendo with examples 2）：
1. **Trademark** – protects signs that distinguish Nintendo's goods and services from others. It is the most suitable for Nintendo's **name, logos and character names** (e.g. "Nintendo", "Super Mario", "Pokémon", "Zelda"), which customers use to identify genuine Nintendo products. Registration (under laws such as Malaysia's Trademarks Act 2019) gives Nintendo exclusive rights, can be renewed indefinitely so the brand is protected long-term, and lets Nintendo stop counterfeit merchandise and look-alike brands that could confuse consumers.
2. **Copyright** – protects original works expressed in physical form. It is the most suitable for Nintendo's **character designs, game software, artwork, music and marketing content**. Protection arises automatically, lasts for a long period (life + 50 years in Malaysia; 70 in many countries), and lets Nintendo stop anyone reproducing, distributing or streaming its games, images or videos without permission — for example pirated game copies or unlicensed use of Mario artwork.

### ✅ 满分答法 — Oct 2024 Q4b: Suggest to Domitri the TWO most suitable types of IPR for his encryption algorithm, with justification. (10 marks)
1. **Patent** (5) – protects a new invention that involves an inventive step and is industrially applicable, giving the owner the exclusive right to exploit it, license it and exclude others for a fixed period (20 years from filing under the Patents Act 1983). It suits Domitri because his real-time encryption method is innovative and widely used: a patent (for the encryption method as applied in a technical system) protects the **functional idea itself**, so no company can use his method — even with different code — without a licence; this matches his wish to permit or restrict usage and to earn licence fees. *(Note: a pure mathematical method may not be patentable on its own, so the application should claim its technical implementation.)*
2. **Copyright** (5) – protects the original expression of his work, i.e. the **source code and software** implementing the algorithm, automatically and without registration, for his life plus 50 years in Malaysia. It lets him stop others from copying, modifying or distributing his software and supports licence agreements with the technology companies that integrate it.
*(Alternative accepted: **trade secret** — keep the algorithm's internal details confidential and license it only under NDAs; justify it, but note the risk that it can be lost if reverse-engineered or leaked, which is why patent is stronger for a widely distributed algorithm.)*

### ✅ 满分答法 — Jan 2025 Q4b: Interpret each type of IP protection relevant to Cadiz and how it could protect the AI-powered shopping portal. (3 + 3 + 2 + 2)
- **(i) Patents (3)** – an exclusive right for a new, inventive and industrially applicable invention for a fixed period. Cadiz can patent a novel technical process in its portal, e.g. its AI recommendation method or a unique checkout technology, so competitors cannot use the same method without a licence.
- **(ii) Copyrights (3)** – protect original works expressed in a physical form, automatically. Cadiz's source code, website layout and graphics, product descriptions and images are protected, so the competitor cannot copy its unique design features and content.
- **(iii) Trademarks (2)** – protect signs that distinguish Cadiz's services. Cadiz should register its brand name, logo and slogan under the Trademarks Act 2019 to stop the competitor using its branding elements.
- **(iv) Trade secrets (2)** – protect confidential information that gives a competitive advantage. Cadiz's AI model parameters, training data and customer analytics should be kept confidential through NDAs and access controls.

### ✅ 满分答法 — May 2026 Q4c / Tutorial 8 Q9b: Describe THREE (FOUR) types of IPR the developers can use to protect their game (app). (6 marks)

2. **Trademark** – registering the game's name and logo prevents other companies from using identical or confusingly similar branding.
3. **Patent** – if the unique gameplay feature is a new, inventive technical method, a patent stops competitors from implementing the same mechanism.

## 4. Issues Related to IP（p28–45）⭐

六个问题（考题要 FIVE，挑最熟的五个）：

### 4.1 Plagiarism（p29–30）
- **偷别人的想法或文字，当成自己的**；剪贴段落到报告中却**没有适当引用或引号**。
- 著名作家、剧作家、歌手、记者，甚至**软件开发者**都有此问题。
- 教育各阶段普遍：虽然有 code of ethics 定义并建议惩罚，许多学生**不太了解什么是抄袭**；有人以为**电子资料都可免费使用**，有人因**分数压力、太累、赶时间**而故意抄。

### 4.2 Reverse engineering（p31–33）
- **拆解检视一个市面上可取得的产品**，以确定其**整体结构、组件、技术**与所有让它运作的特征。
- 对手可借此**发现生产与设计秘密**，用于自己的发明。
- **在马来西亚违法吗？**（p32–33）马来西亚**没有直接禁止** reverse engineering 的法律，但以下框架可能**间接限制**：**Copyright Act 1987**（复制 code 可能侵权）、**Patents Act 1983**（用逆向得来的知识制造专利产品仍侵权）、**Trade secrets / confidential information**（若以不正当方式取得）、**EULA**（软件授权协议常禁止 reverse engineering，违反即违约）。

### 4.3 Open source code（p34–36）
- **Open-source software** = 原始码**公开**，任何人都可**存取、修改、再发布**；**closed-source** 的原始码不公开或只在严格保密条件下披露。
- 例：**Google Chrome 介面**与 **Netflix** 的技术工具都建基于 open-source 技术。
- 好处：公开发布并**受公众审查**，对开发者与科技公司是强大有用的资源。
- **IP 问题**：使用前要**仔细研究 license 条款**（有些要求衍生作品也必须开源）；若想做**专有 (proprietary) 软件**并保持原始码私有，可联络拥有者看能否以**royalty** 换取 proprietary license。

### 4.4 Competitive intelligence（p37–38）
- 申请 patent 等 IPR 可能**耗时耗钱**。
- **Competitive intelligence (CI)** = **发现、分析、收集专利与市场资料**的过程。
- 目的：了解行业进展与对手的**强弱、创新、IP**；收集对手的**策略、产品、下一步**；识别**商业风险与机会**，为成长策略提供市场资料。
- 在 IPR 领域：协助维持/扩大市场占有率、技术评估、取得授权、监控主要对手、解决技术挑战。
- *Extra — 伦理界线*：CI 应使用**合法公开**的来源（专利资料库、年报、展览）；若用贿赂、窃取、冒充取得对手机密，就变成**产业间谍 (industrial espionage)**，侵犯 trade secret。

### 4.5 Trademark infringement（p39）
- **非法使用**商标或服务标章；可能发生在商品或服务上，导致**错误资讯、欺骗、或对产品/服务真正来源的混淆**。
- 商标拥有者可以**提告**；若证明滥用，法院会发出 **injunction**（禁止令）禁止侵权者使用，拥有者可获**金钱赔偿**。

### 4.6 Cybersquatting（p40–45）
- **注册与第三方企业名称或商标相同或相近的 Internet domain**，以**利用该品牌的商誉 (goodwill)**，或**高价转卖**。
- 网络骗徒用知名企业名称注册网站赚大钱。
- 目的：把 domain **卖回给商标拥有者**获利、利用第三方品牌商誉，或进行**网络犯罪**（phishing 网站、诈骗、假问卷）。

**四种 cybersquatting（p42–45）**：

| Type | 说明 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| **Typosquatting** | 注册知名品牌/网站的**拼错版本**；只改一个字母或加连字号；利用**用户打错字** | **Googgle.com** vs Google.com；`cadizz.com` |
| **Identity theft** | 注册看起来**与目标一模一样**的 domain，**冒充合法企业的网上身份**；用户想进公司网站却误入 **phishing domain** | `cadiz-shop.com.my` 仿冒 Cadiz 官网骗登入资料 |
| **Name jacking** | 用**名人的真实姓名**建立假网站或**社交媒体帐户** | 注册某明星名字的 domain 或 Instagram 帐号 |
| **Reverse cybersquatting (reverse domain name hijacking)** | 反过来：攻击者利用规则，**先成立与已有网站同名的公司**（如针对 InfinityFinance.com 成立 Infinity Finance Ltd），再**指控真正的 domain 拥有者在 cybersquatting**，透过法律**夺取**该网站 | 新公司"Cadiz Holdings"成立后，声称 Cadiz Sdn. Bhd. 的 domain 侵犯其名称，要求转让 |

### ✅ 满分答法 — Jan 2025 Q4c: Explain to Cadiz the types of cybersquatting: (i) reverse-cybersquatting (3) (ii) typosquatting (3) (iii) name jacking (2) (iv) identity theft (2).
- **(i) Reverse cybersquatting (3)** – also called reverse domain name hijacking: a party abuses the domain-dispute rules to take a legitimately registered domain from its owner. They pick an established website, register a company with the same name, then falsely accuse the genuine owner of cybersquatting and use legal action to take the domain. *For Cadiz:* a newly registered company with a similar name could try to seize Cadiz's portal domain through a complaint; Cadiz should keep records proving its earlier, legitimate use of the name.
- **(ii) Typosquatting (3)** – registering misspelled versions of a popular domain (one letter changed or a hyphen added) to capture users who mistype the address, e.g. Googgle.com. *For Cadiz:* squatters may register "cadlz.com" or "cadiz-shop.com" to show fake stores or ads to its customers; Cadiz should register common misspellings and monitor new registrations.
- **(iii) Name jacking (2)** – registering domains or social media accounts using the real name of a famous person to create fake sites. *For Cadiz:* squatters could impersonate Cadiz's founder or celebrity ambassadors on fake social media accounts to mislead customers.
- **(iv) Identity theft (2)** – registering a domain that looks identical to the business's to assume its online identity; users trying to reach the real site land on a phishing domain. *For Cadiz:* a look-alike domain could copy Cadiz's portal to steal customers' login and payment details.

### ✅ 满分答法 — May 2025 Q4c / Oct 2025 Q4c: Briefly explain FIVE issues related to intellectual property protection. (10 marks)
2 分一个（定义 + 为什么是 IP 问题/例子）：
1. **Plagiarism** – stealing someone else's ideas or words and presenting them as one's own, e.g. copying Internet passages into a report without attribution; common in education and even among software developers.
2. **Reverse engineering** – examining and dismantling a commercially available product to discover its structure, components and technology; rivals can learn design and production secrets for their own products. Malaysia has no law directly prohibiting it, but the Copyright Act, Patents Act, trade secret law and EULAs may restrict it.
3. **Open source code** – software whose source code is publicly available to access, modify and redistribute; users must follow the licence terms, and companies wishing to keep derived code proprietary must obtain permission or a proprietary licence.
4. **Competitive intelligence** – discovering, analysing and gathering patent and market data about competitors' strategies, products and IP; it is legitimate when using public sources but becomes an IP issue if confidential information is obtained improperly.
5. **Trademark infringement** – unauthorised use of a trademark or service mark that causes confusion about the true source of goods or services; the owner can sue for an injunction and compensation.
6. **Cybersquatting** – registering domain names identical or similar to another company's name or trademark to profit from its goodwill or resell it; types include typosquatting, identity theft, name jacking and reverse cybersquatting.

### ✅ 满分答法 — May 2026 Q4e: Explain why downloading pirated digital games is considered an IP violation. (3 marks)
A game is protected by **copyright**, which gives the owner the exclusive right to reproduce and distribute it (1). Downloading a pirated copy makes an unauthorised reproduction obtained without the owner's permission or payment, which infringes that exclusive right under laws such as Malaysia's Copyright Act 1987 (1). ## Cheat sheet

- **IP** = creations of the mind（inventions, literary/artistic works, symbols/names/images/designs in commerce）；intangible 但可能比实体资产值钱。
- **IPR** 重要：exclusive commercial benefit；prevent unauthorised use；sue for injunction + compensation；competitive advantage；valuable asset；encourage innovation。
- **做法 → patent；名字 → trademark；作品 → copyright；秘密 → trade secret。**
- **Patent**：new + inventive step + industrially applicable；Patents Act 1983 & Regulations 1986；exploit / assign / license；**20 年自申请日**（slide：15 年自授权日）。
- **Trademark**：Trademarks Act 2019；5 spectrums：**Generic (CLOCK) → Descriptive (COLD AND CREAMY) → Suggestive (SWEETARTS) → Arbitrary (BLACKBERRY) → Fanciful (KODAK)**，由弱到强。
- **Copyright**：expressed physically；Copyright Act 1987（Berne）；Amendments 2012, 2022（streaming）；**Malaysia life + 50**，SG/US/EU life + 70；好处 = encourage creativity + global distribution。
- **Trade secret** = confidential information；不能注册；Competition Act 2010、Penal Code；internal（employee agreements, access control, everyday practices）+ external（**NDA**）。
- **6 issues**：plagiarism, reverse engineering（Malaysia 无直接禁止；Copyright Act, Patents Act, trade secrets, EULA）, open source（守 license）, competitive intelligence, trademark infringement（injunction + compensation）, cybersquatting。
- **Cybersquatting 4 types**：typosquatting（Googgle.com）、identity theft（look-alike phishing domain）、name jacking（名人名字）、reverse cybersquatting（滥用法律夺 domain）。
- **侵权后**：evidence → cease-and-desist / takedown → lawsuit（injunction + damages）→ report to MyIPO/KPDN。
- **Piracy** = 未授权复制 → 侵犯 copyright 的独占复制与发行权。

### A. MCQ

1. "Kodak" is an example of which trademark spectrum?
   (a) Descriptive (b) Suggestive (c) Arbitrary (d) Fanciful/coined
2. In Malaysia, copyright in a literary work lasts for:
   (a) 20 years from publication (b) life of the author + 50 years (c) life + 70 years (d) 15 years from grant
3. Which IP cannot be registered in Malaysia?
   (a) Patent (b) Trademark (c) Trade secret (d) Industrial design
4. Registering "amazzon.com" to capture mistyped traffic is:
   (a) Name jacking (b) Typosquatting (c) Reverse cybersquatting (d) Competitive intelligence
5. A new feature that lets a game unlock levels by scanning real food is best protected by:
   (a) Trademark (b) Copyright only (c) Patent (if novel and inventive) (d) Trade secret only

**Answers:** 1-d, 2-b, 3-c, 4-b, 5-c

### B. Short answer

**B1. State the three conditions for an invention to be patentable in Malaysia. (3 marks)** It must be new; it must involve an inventive step; it must be industrially applicable.

**B2. Differentiate copyright and patent. (4 marks)** Copyright protects the original expression of a work (code, art, music) automatically upon creation and lasts for the author's life plus 50 years in Malaysia; it does not protect the underlying idea. A patent protects a new, inventive, industrially applicable invention (the functional idea/method itself), must be applied for and granted, and lasts for a limited period (20 years from filing).

**B3. Give THREE internal measures to protect trade secrets. (3 marks)** Strengthen employee agreements (confidentiality clauses); implement access controls; address everyday practices (labelling, training, clean-desk, return of materials on leaving).

### C. Application

*Case: GreenBite Sdn. Bhd. develops "NutriScan", an AI app that estimates calories from food photos.*

**C1.** Recommend the most suitable IPR for each: (a) the app name "NutriScan" and logo, (b) the app's source code and interface graphics, (c) the trained AI model weights and training data, (d) a novel image-processing method for estimating portion size. (8 marks)
(a) Trademark — distinguishes GreenBite's app; register under the Trademarks Act 2019. (b) Copyright — original code and graphics protected automatically. (c) Trade secret — keep confidential with access controls and NDAs; not disclosed publicly. (d) Patent — if new, inventive and industrially applicable, it protects the method itself from being used by competitors.

**C2.** A competitor decompiles NutriScan to study its algorithms. Is this legal in Malaysia? (4 marks)
Malaysia has no law directly prohibiting reverse engineering, but it may be restricted indirectly: copying the decompiled code infringes the Copyright Act 1987; using the knowledge to make a product covered by GreenBite's patent infringes the Patents Act 1983; if the EULA prohibits reverse engineering, the competitor breaches the contract; and improperly obtaining confidential information may breach trade secret protection.

**C3.** Someone registers "nutriscan-login.com" and copies GreenBite's login page. Identify the type of cybersquatting and TWO actions GreenBite should take. (4 marks)
Identity theft (a look-alike phishing domain assuming GreenBite's online identity). Actions: file a domain dispute/complaint to have the domain suspended or transferred and report the phishing site to the hosting provider/authorities; warn users and register likely look-alike domains defensively.

### D. Thinking

**D1.** Is open-source software a threat to intellectual property? (4 marks)
Not necessarily. Open source is itself based on copyright licences that set conditions for use, modification and redistribution; it promotes innovation because code is publicly reviewed and improved (e.g. Chrome and Netflix tools). The IP risk arises only when users ignore licence terms — e.g. including open-source code in proprietary software without complying — so companies must check licences or obtain a proprietary licence.

**D2.** "Plagiarism is only a problem in schools." Discuss. (4 marks)
No. The slide notes that writers, playwrights, singers, journalists and software developers also plagiarise — e.g. copying code from others without attribution or licence. Plagiarism in business can lead to copyright infringement lawsuits and reputational damage, so organisations need codes of ethics, attribution rules and code-scanning tools.
