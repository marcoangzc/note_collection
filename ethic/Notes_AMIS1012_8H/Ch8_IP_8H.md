## 1. What is Intellectual Property? 什么是知识产权

### ❓ 一首歌、一个 logo、一段 code，摸不到，为什么也算"财产"？

**大白话**
财产不一定是摸得到的东西（房子、车子）。**脑袋想出来、创作出来的东西**也可以是财产，例如：一个新发明、一首歌、一幅画、一个品牌名字。这些叫 **Intellectual Property（IP，知识产权）**。

它们为什么值钱？想想 **Nintendo 的 "Super Mario"**：这个角色可以拿来卖游戏、玩具、电影，赚的钱可能比 Nintendo 所有办公室和工厂加起来还多。所以 IP **可能比实体资产（physical assets）更值钱**。

📖 **讲师原句**
- *Definition:* "**Creations of the mind** (**inventions, art, designs, brand names**)."
- *Why important:* "Can be **more valuable than physical assets**. **Protects innovation** and creates **competitive advantage**."

| English | 中文 | 一句话 |
|---|---|---|
| **Intellectual property (IP)** | 知识产权 | 脑袋的创作：发明、艺术、设计、品牌名 |
| **Physical assets** | 实体资产 | 摸得到的：大楼、机器、电脑 |
| **Innovation** | 创新 | 新的想法、新的发明 |
| **Competitive advantage** | 竞争优势 | 你有、对手没有，让你赢过对手的东西 |

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *Intellectual property refers to creations of the mind, such as inventions, art, designs and brand names. It can be more valuable than physical assets, and protecting it protects innovation and creates a competitive advantage.*

## 2. Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) 知识产权权利

### ❓ 2.1 有了 IP，法律给创作者什么"权利"？

**大白话**
如果你辛苦写了一首歌，别人随便拿去卖，你一毛钱都拿不到，你还会继续创作吗？所以法律给创作者一些**权利（rights）**：
- 只有你可以**用它赚钱**（卖、授权给别人用）；
- 别人要用，**必须先问你**；
- 有人偷用，你可以**去法院告他**，要他停止并赔钱。

这些就是 **Intellectual Property Rights（IPR）**。

📖 **讲师原句**
- "**Rights given to creators** to **protect and benefit** from their work."
- "**Prevents unauthorized use**."
- "Examples: **patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets**."

### ❓ 2.2 为什么 IPR 那么重要？⭐（五份卷都考！）

背这 **5 个理由**，考试挑 3–4 个，每个都要**套 case 的公司**：

| Reason | 中文 | 大白话 |
|---|---|---|
| **Exclusive right to benefit / earn income** | 独家获利 | 只有创作者能卖、能授权（licence）别人用来收钱 |
| **Prevents unauthorised use** | 阻止别人乱用 | 别人要用，一定要先得到许可 |
| **Legal protection** | 法律保护 | 被抄可以告，要求法院下**禁止令（injunction）**和**赔偿（compensation）** |
| **Competitive advantage** | 竞争优势 | 对手抄不走你的发明和品牌 → 保住市场、品牌价值、吸引投资者 |
| **Protects / encourages innovation** | 鼓励创新 | 付出有回报，才愿意继续投钱研发、继续创作 |

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *IPR are rights given to creators to protect and benefit from their work. They are important because they give the owner the exclusive right to earn income from the work, prevent unauthorised use, allow legal action against infringers for an injunction and compensation, create a competitive advantage, and encourage further innovation.*

### ✅ 真题满分答案

**May 2025 Q4a** — *Examine why Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) are important to Nintendo. Provide relevant examples.* **(5 marks)**

> 1. **Exclusive right to earn income:** IPR give Nintendo the exclusive right to benefit from its games, consoles and characters. For example, only Nintendo and the companies it licenses can sell *Super Mario* games, toys and clothing, which brings in large licensing income. (2)
> 2. **Prevents unauthorised use:** IPR prevent others from using Nintendo's names, logos and characters without permission. For example, a shop selling unlicensed Pokémon plush toys can be stopped. (1)
> 3. **Legal protection:** if its rights are infringed, Nintendo can take legal action for an injunction and compensation. For example, Nintendo can sue websites that share pirated Nintendo Switch games. (1)
> 4. **Competitive advantage:** Nintendo's characters and brand are worth more than its physical assets. Because competitors cannot copy them, customers can only get the genuine experience from Nintendo, which protects its market position. (1)

**Oct 2024 Q4a** — *Domitri, a software developer, created a real-time encryption algorithm that is widely adopted by technology companies. He wants to control who can use it. Examine why IPR are important to Domitri.* **(5 marks)**

> 1. **Exclusive right to benefit:** the encryption algorithm is Domitri's own creation; IPR give him the exclusive right to earn income from it, for example by charging licence fees to the technology companies that use it. (2)
> 2. **Control over use:** IPR let Domitri permit or restrict the use of his algorithm — a company must get his permission, and agree to his terms, before integrating it into its products. (1)
> 3. **Legal protection:** if someone uses the algorithm without permission, Domitri can take legal action to stop them and claim compensation. (1)
> 4. **Competitive advantage and innovation:** because the algorithm is widely adopted, it is a very valuable asset; protecting it stops competitors from copying it and encourages Domitri to keep improving and creating new technology. (1)

**Jan 2025 Q4a** — *Cadiz launched an AI-powered shopping portal, but a competitor soon copied its unique design features and branding elements. Determine the importance of protecting IPR for Cadiz and discuss how IPR contribute to its competitive advantage.* **(5 marks)**

> **Importance (3):** Cadiz's AI shopping portal, its design features and its branding are creations of the mind that Cadiz spent a lot of money and effort to develop. IPR give Cadiz the exclusive right to use and benefit from them, prevent the competitor from using them without permission, and allow Cadiz to take legal action for an injunction and compensation — which it needs now that the competitor has copied its design and branding.
>
> **Competitive advantage (2):** with its IP protected, Cadiz's AI features and brand remain unique, so customers can only get that experience from Cadiz. This keeps Cadiz different from competitors, protects its market share and reputation, and lets it earn licensing income and attract investors.

**Oct 2025 Q4a** — *Disney created the characters of Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs. Explain how Disney would apply its IPR to protect its contributions throughout the production and distribution process. Provide a specific example for each.* **(5 marks)**

> - **Production – copyright and trade secrets (2):** Disney's scripts, character drawings, animation and music are protected by copyright as soon as they are created. Unreleased stories and production methods are kept as trade secrets, protected by non-disclosure agreements. *Example:* animators working on a new film sign NDAs, so the plot and character designs cannot be leaked before release.
> - **New technology – patents (1):** new animation or theme-park technology that is new, inventive and industrially applicable can be patented. *Example:* a patented animation technique cannot be used by rival studios without a licence.
> - **Distribution – trademarks and licensing (2):** character names, logos and the Disney brand are registered trademarks used on films, streaming and merchandise, and Disney licenses them to approved partners. *Example:* only licensed companies may sell toys with the "Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs" name and logo.

**May 2026 Q4a** — *A small team of indie developers created a game with original characters, music, logo and gameplay. It went viral, and competitors started copying its design and branding. Explain TWO reasons why IPR are important for the game creators.* **(6 marks)**

> 1. **Exclusive right to earn income from their work (3):** the characters, music, logo and gameplay are the developers' own creations. IPR give them the exclusive right to sell the game, sell merchandise and license the characters, so they are rewarded for their effort. Now that the game has gone viral, this income is at risk if competitors can copy freely, and protection encourages the team to keep creating.
> 2. **Legal power to stop copying and protect their brand (3):** IPR prevent unauthorised use and let the developers take legal action against competitors who copy the game's design and branding, asking the court for an injunction and compensation. This protects their market share and stops players from being confused by imitation games that could damage the original game's reputation.

## 3. Types of IP 四种知识产权 ⭐

### ❓ 发明、品牌名、一首歌、一个秘密配方，都用同一种方法保护吗？

**不是**。不同的东西用不同的 IP。先背这个口诀：

> **做法 → Patent · 名字 → Trademark · 作品 → Copyright · 秘密 → Trade secret**

| 要保护的东西 | 用 | 中文 | 🌰 讲师例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 新的**发明**、新技术、新方法 | **Patent** | 专利 | **Apple** patents **iPhone touchscreen** technology |
| **品牌名、logo**、slogan | **Trademark** | 商标 | **Nike "Swoosh"** logo |
| **作品**：歌、小说、画、**software code** | **Copyright** | 版权 | **A song, novel, or software code** |
| **不公开的商业资讯** | **Trade secret** | 商业秘密 | **Coca-Cola recipe** |

### ❓ 3.1 Patent（专利）：新发明怎么保护？

**大白话**
你发明了一个新东西（例如新的手机触控技术），去政府**申请专利**。批准后，**在一段时间内只有你可以做、卖、授权**这个发明，别人就算自己想到一样的方法也不能用。但要拿到专利，发明要符合 **3 个条件**：
1. **New**（新的）：以前没有人公开过；
2. **Inventive**（有创造性）：不是一般人一想就知道的；
3. **Industrially applicable**（能在工业上应用）：真的能做出来、用得上。

📖 **讲师原句**
- "**Protect inventions**."
- "In Malaysia → Governed by **Patents Act 1983**."
- "Requirements: **New, inventive, and industrially applicable**."
- "Valid for **15 years from grant**."
- 🌰 "**Apple patents iPhone touchscreen technology**."

⚠️ 讲师写 **15 年**（这是旧条文），现在的法律是**从申请日起 20 年**。考试**照讲师写 15 年**，可以在括号补 *"(current law: 20 years from filing)"*。

### ❓ 3.2 Trademark（商标）：品牌名怎么保护？

**大白话**
你在店里看到那个"勾勾"，马上知道是 Nike。**Trademark 就是让顾客分得出"这是哪一家的东西"的标志**：名字、logo、slogan。注册了商标，别人就不能用一样或很像的标志，免得顾客被骗。

📖 **讲师原句**
- "**Distinguish goods/services from competitors**."
- "Governed by **Trademarks Act 2019**."
- 🌰 "**Nike 'Swoosh'** logo."

### ❓ 3.3 为什么 "Kodak" 很容易注册成商标，"Milk" 却几乎不可能？（5 spectrum）

**大白话**
"Milk" 就是牛奶本身的名字，每一家卖牛奶的都要用，**不能让一家公司独占**。"Kodak" 是自己造出来的字，没有其他意思，**只代表这一家公司**，所以最容易保护。

商标按照**有多独特（distinctiveness）**分成 5 级，从最弱到最强：

```
  最弱 WEAK ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────→ 最强 STRONG
  很难注册                                                         最容易注册、最容易保护

  Generic       Descriptive       Suggestive       Arbitrary          Fanciful / Coined
  通用名称       直接描述           暗示              无关的普通字         自己造的字
  "Milk"        "Fast Cleaners"   "Netflix"        "Apple"            "Kodak"
                                                   （卖电脑）
```

| Spectrum | 中文 | 大白话 | 📖 讲师例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Generic** | 通用名称 | 就是产品本身的名字 | **"Milk"**（weakest） |
| **Descriptive** | 描述性 | 直接说出产品是什么、做什么 | **"Fast Cleaners"**（洗衣店） |
| **Suggestive** | 暗示性 | 暗示产品特点，要想一下才明白 | **"Netflix"**（net + flicks = 网上看电影） |
| **Arbitrary** | 任意性 | 一个普通的字，但和产品完全无关 | **"Apple"** for computers |
| **Fanciful / Coined** | 臆造性 | 自己发明的新字，没有其他意思 | **"Kodak"**（strongest） |

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *Trademarks fall on a spectrum of distinctiveness: generic (the common name of the product, e.g. "Milk", the weakest), descriptive (directly describes the product, e.g. "Fast Cleaners"), suggestive (hints at a quality, e.g. "Netflix"), arbitrary (a common word used for an unrelated product, e.g. "Apple" for computers) and fanciful or coined (an invented word, e.g. "Kodak", the strongest).*

### ❓ 3.4 Copyright（版权）：歌、小说、程式码怎么保护？

**大白话**
你写了一首歌、一本小说、一段程式码，**一写出来就自动受版权保护，不用去注册**。别人不能未经你同意复制、发布、卖。
但要注意：版权保护的是**你写出来的样子（表达方式）**，不保护**想法**。别人也可以写一个"扫描食物解锁关卡"的游戏，只要不是抄你的 code 和美术。想法要保护，要用 patent。

📖 **讲师原句**
- "Protects **artistic/literary works**."
- "In Malaysia → **Copyright Act 1987**, updated **2012 & 2022**."
- "Lasts **life of creator + 50 years**."
- "Based on international conventions (**Berne Convention**)."
- 🌰 "**A song, novel, or software code**."

### ❓ 3.5 Trade Secret（商业秘密）：秘密配方怎么保护？

**大白话**
Coca-Cola 的配方一百多年来都没有申请专利（因为申请专利要公开内容，而且有期限），而是**一直保密**。只要秘密没有外泄，就一直受保护。
马来西亚**没有专门的 trade secret 法律**，所以要靠**合约**来保护：签保密协议、限制谁能看、在员工合约里写明不能泄露。

📖 **讲师原句**
- "**Confidential info giving business advantage** (e.g., **Coca-Cola recipe**)."
- "In Malaysia → **Not a specific law**, but protected under **contracts and common law**."
- "Protection via **NDAs, access control, employee agreements**."

| 保护方法 | 中文 | 大白话 | 🌰 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NDA**（Non-disclosure agreement） | 保密协议 | 给别人看之前，先签约答应不说出去 | 合作伙伴看 source code 前先签 NDA |
| **Access control** | 存取控制 | 只有需要知道的人才能看 | 只有 3 个核心工程师能打开原始码 |
| **Employee agreements** | 员工合约 | 合约写明不能泄露、离职后也不能带走 | 离职员工不能把客户名单带去对手公司 |

### 四种 IP 总比较

| | **Patent** | **Trademark** | **Copyright** | **Trade secret** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 保护什么 | Inventions | Brand names, logos | Artistic / literary works, code | Confidential business info |
| 马来西亚法律 | Patents Act 1983 | Trademarks Act 2019 | Copyright Act 1987 (2012, 2022) | No specific law → contracts & common law |
| 要注册吗 | 要 | 要 | 不用（自动） | 不能注册，靠保密 |
| 多久 | 15 years from grant（讲师） | 可以一直续期 | Life + 50 years | 只要保密就一直有效 |
| 讲师例子 | Apple touchscreen | Nike Swoosh | Song, novel, code | Coca-Cola recipe |

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *A patent protects inventions that are new, inventive and industrially applicable, under the Patents Act 1983, for 15 years from grant. A trademark distinguishes goods or services from competitors, under the Trademarks Act 2019. Copyright protects artistic and literary works, including software code, under the Copyright Act 1987, for the life of the creator plus 50 years. A trade secret is confidential information giving a business advantage, protected by contracts, NDAs, access control and employee agreements.*

### ❓ 3.6 考试叫你"选最适合的两种 IPR 并 justify"，怎么写？

**方法**：看 case 里**要保护的是什么东西**，用口诀对上；每一种写 5 分：
**名字 (1) + 它保护什么、法律、期限 (2) + 为什么适合这个 case，给具体例子 (2)**

### ✅ 真题满分答案

**May 2025 Q4b** — *Suggest TWO most suitable types of IPR that Nintendo should consider for its market content, with justification.* **(10 marks)**

> **1. Trademark (5)** – A trademark distinguishes a company's goods and services from its competitors and, in Malaysia, is governed by the Trademarks Act 2019. It is the most suitable for Nintendo's **names and logos** — "Nintendo", "Super Mario", "Pokémon" and "Zelda" — because customers use these names to recognise genuine Nintendo products. Registration gives Nintendo the exclusive right to use them, can be renewed so the brand is protected for as long as Nintendo uses it, and lets Nintendo stop fake merchandise and look-alike brands that could confuse customers.
>
> **2. Copyright (5)** – Copyright protects artistic and literary works, including software code, and in Malaysia lasts for the life of the creator plus 50 years under the Copyright Act 1987. It is the most suitable for Nintendo's **character designs, game software, artwork and music**, because it protects them automatically without registration. It allows Nintendo to stop anyone from copying, distributing or streaming its games, images or music without permission — for example, pirated copies of Switch games or unlicensed use of Mario artwork.

**Oct 2024 Q4b** — *Suggest to Domitri the TWO most suitable types of IPR for his encryption algorithm, with justification.* **(10 marks)**

> **1. Patent (5)** – A patent protects inventions that are new, inventive and industrially applicable; in Malaysia it is governed by the Patents Act 1983 and is valid for 15 years from grant (current law: 20 years from filing). It suits Domitri because his real-time encryption method is new and inventive and is widely used in industry. A patent protects the **method itself**, so no company can use it — even with different code — without his licence. This matches his wish to permit or restrict usage and to earn licence fees.
>
> **2. Copyright (5)** – Copyright protects literary works, including software code, automatically, for the life of the creator plus 50 years under the Copyright Act 1987. It suits Domitri because the **source code** that implements his algorithm is his original work. Copyright lets him stop others from copying, modifying or distributing his software, and supports licence agreements with the companies that integrate it.
>
> *(Also acceptable: **trade secret** — keep the internal details of the algorithm confidential and share them only under NDAs; but the secret could be lost if it leaks, which is why a patent is stronger for a widely used algorithm.)*

**Jan 2025 Q4b** — *Interpret each type of IP protection relevant to Cadiz and how it could protect the AI-powered shopping portal: (i) Patents (3) (ii) Copyrights (3) (iii) Trademarks (2) (iv) Trade secrets (2).* **(10 marks)**

> **(i) Patents (3):** a patent protects an invention that is new, inventive and industrially applicable, under the Patents Act 1983. Cadiz could patent a new technical method in its portal, such as its AI recommendation process, so competitors cannot use the same method without a licence.
>
> **(ii) Copyrights (3):** copyright automatically protects artistic and literary works, including software code, for the life of the creator plus 50 years. Cadiz's source code, website design, graphics, product descriptions and images are protected, so the competitor cannot copy its unique design features.
>
> **(iii) Trademarks (2):** a trademark distinguishes Cadiz's services from its competitors. Cadiz should register its brand name, logo and slogan under the Trademarks Act 2019 to stop the competitor using similar branding elements.
>
> **(iv) Trade secrets (2):** a trade secret is confidential information that gives a business advantage. Cadiz should keep its AI model settings, training data and customer analysis confidential through NDAs, access control and employee agreements.

**Oct 2025 Q4b** — *Edward's company XYZ released an app featuring a dwarf character that closely resembles one of Disney's Seven Dwarfs. If Disney took legal action, describe how Disney could use TWO relevant types of IPR to address the infringement. For each, provide ONE concrete action or claim.* **(10 marks)**

> **1. Copyright (5)** – Disney owns the copyright in its artistic drawings of the Seven Dwarfs — their specific look, costumes and style — which is protected as an artistic work for the life of the creator plus 50 years in Malaysia. Edward's dwarf closely resembles one of them, so it is an unauthorised copy of Disney's artistic work. *Action:* Disney sues XYZ for copyright infringement, asking the court for an **injunction** requiring the app to remove the character and for **compensation** for the profits XYZ made from it. *(Note: the old Snow White fairy tale itself is public; what is protected is Disney's own drawings of the characters.)*
>
> **2. Trademark (5)** – Disney's character names and logos are registered trademarks that tell customers a product comes from Disney. If the app's dwarf uses a similar name or look that makes users think the app is made or approved by Disney, customers are confused about the source. *Action:* Disney sends XYZ a **cease-and-desist letter** demanding that it stop using the look-alike character and any similar name in the app and its advertising, and files a trademark infringement claim if XYZ does not comply.

**May 2026 Q4c** — *Describe THREE types of IPR the developers can use to protect their game.* **(6 marks)**

> 1. **Copyright (2)** – automatically protects the game's original characters, artwork, music, story and source code for the life of the creator plus 50 years, so others cannot copy or distribute them.
> 2. **Trademark (2)** – registering the game's name and logo under the Trademarks Act 2019 stops competitors from using the same or confusingly similar branding.
> 3. **Patent (2)** – if the game's unique gameplay feature is a new, inventive and industrially applicable technical method, a patent stops competitors from using the same mechanism.

## 4. Issues in IP 知识产权的问题 ⭐

### ❓ 就算有法律保护，现实中 IP 还会遇到哪些麻烦？

讲师列了 **6 个**。考题通常要 **FIVE**，挑你最熟的 5 个。

### 4.1 Plagiarism 抄袭
**大白话**：把别人的文字、想法、程式码**当成自己的**，没有注明出处。在学校、写作、写程式时都很常见。
📖 "**Copying without credit**. Common in **schools, writing, software**."
🌰 学生从网上复制一段文字放进 assignment，没有 cite；程式员从 GitHub 复制整段 code 说是自己写的。

### 4.2 Reverse Engineering 逆向工程
**大白话**：买一个别人的产品回来，**拆开研究**它是怎么做的，再做一个类似的。有时合法（例如为了让自己的产品能和它兼容），但**道德上有争议**。
📖 "**Disassembling products to copy designs**. **Legal in some contexts but ethically debated**."
🌰 一家公司买了对手的新手机，拆开研究芯片和电路设计，做出几乎一样的手机。

### 4.3 Open Source vs Closed Source 开源 vs 闭源
**大白话**：**Open source** 的程式码是公开的，任何人都可以免费用、改、再发布；**closed source** 的程式码是公司私有的，不给看。问题在于：用开源 code 时要**遵守它的 licence**（有些要求你改过的版本也要公开），否则就是侵权。
📖 "Open = **free to use & modify** (**Linux**). Closed = **proprietary** (**Windows**)."
🌰 一家公司把开源 code 放进自己要卖的软件里，却没有照 licence 公开修改后的 code。

### 4.4 Competitive Intelligence (CI) 竞争情报
**大白话**：收集和分析**对手的专利和市场资料**，了解他们在做什么。用公开的资料（专利资料库、年报、展览）是合法的；但如果用偷、骗、贿赂的方式拿对手的机密，就变成**商业间谍**，侵犯 trade secret。
📖 "**Gathering patent/market data to monitor competitors**."
🌰 一家手机公司每月查对手的新专利，预测对手明年会推出什么功能。

### 4.5 Trademark Infringement 商标侵权
**大白话**：没有得到许可，**用了别人的商标**（或很像的商标），让顾客以为是正牌货。
📖 "**Using another's mark illegally** (**fake Adidas shoes**)."
🌰 夜市卖印着 Adidas 三条线的假鞋。商标主人可以告，要求法院下 **injunction**（禁止令）并赔偿。

### 4.6 Cybersquatting 网域抢注
**大白话**：抢先**注册有名品牌的网址（domain name）**，再高价卖回给那个品牌，或者拿来骗人（做假网站）。
📖 "**Registering domain names of famous brands** for **profit or fraud**."
🌰 有人抢先注册 "nintendo-shop.com"，要求 Nintendo 付 RM50,000 买回。

**Cybersquatting 的 4 种类型**（讲师没写，但 Jan 2025 考了 10 分）

| Type | 中文 | 大白话 | 🌰 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Typosquatting** | 错字抢注 | 注册**拼错字**的网址，等人打错 | **Googgle.com**（多一个 g） |
| **Identity theft** | 身份冒充 | 注册**看起来一模一样**的网站，骗人输入密码（phishing） | 假的 "cadiz-shop.com.my" 骗登入资料 |
| **Name jacking** | 名人名字 | 用**名人的名字**开假网站或假社交帐号 | 冒用某明星名字的 Instagram 帐号 |
| **Reverse cybersquatting** | 反向抢注 | 先开一间**同名公司**，再**反告**真正的网址主人抢注，用法律夺走网址 | 新公司 "Cadiz Holdings" 声称 Cadiz 的网址侵权 |

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *Issues in IP include plagiarism (copying without credit), reverse engineering (disassembling products to copy designs, legal in some contexts but ethically debated), open source versus closed source software (open source such as Linux is free to use and modify, while closed source such as Windows is proprietary), competitive intelligence (gathering patent and market data to monitor competitors), trademark infringement (using another's mark illegally, e.g. fake Adidas shoes) and cybersquatting (registering domain names of famous brands for profit or fraud).*

### ✅ 真题满分答案

**May 2025 Q4c / Oct 2025 Q4c** — *Briefly explain FIVE issues related to intellectual property protection.* **(10 marks)**

> 1. **Plagiarism (2)** – copying someone else's words, ideas or code without giving credit. It is common in schools, writing and software development. *Example:* a student copies paragraphs from a website into an assignment without citing the source.
> 2. **Reverse engineering (2)** – disassembling a product to discover how it works and copy its design. It is legal in some contexts but ethically debated. *Example:* a company takes apart a competitor's new smartphone to copy its circuit design.
> 3. **Open source vs closed source (2)** – open source software such as Linux is free to use and modify, while closed source software such as Windows is proprietary. Users of open source code must follow its licence terms. *Example:* a company puts open source code into a product it sells without following the licence, which is an infringement.
> 4. **Trademark infringement (2)** – using another company's trademark, or a confusingly similar one, without permission, which misleads customers about where a product comes from. *Example:* selling fake Adidas shoes; Adidas can sue for an injunction and compensation.
> 5. **Cybersquatting (2)** – registering domain names of famous brands for profit or fraud, e.g. to sell them back at a high price or to host fake websites. *Example:* someone registers "nintendo-shop.com" and demands a large payment from Nintendo.
>
> *(Also acceptable: **competitive intelligence** – gathering patent and market data to monitor competitors; it is acceptable from public sources but becomes an IP issue if confidential information is obtained dishonestly.)*

**May 2026 Q4b** — *Explain TWO IP issues that may occur if the developers fail to protect their game.* **(6 marks)**

> 1. **Trademark infringement (3)** – without a registered trademark, competitors can use the same or a very similar game name, logo and character designs. Players may download the copy thinking it is the original game, so the developers lose revenue and the copy's poor quality damages the original game's reputation. Without registration, it is also harder for the developers to stop them in court.
> 2. **Cybersquatting (3)** – because the game has gone viral, people may register domain names such as the game's name ".com", or misspelt versions of it, before the developers do. They may try to sell the domain back at a high price, or use it for fake download pages that spread malware or steal players' login details, which harms both players and the game's brand.

**Jan 2025 Q4c** — *Explain to Cadiz the types of cybersquatting: (i) reverse-cybersquatting (3) (ii) typosquatting (3) (iii) name jacking (2) (iv) identity theft (2).* **(10 marks)**

> **(i) Reverse cybersquatting (3)** – someone misuses the domain-dispute rules to take a legitimate domain away from its real owner. They choose an established website, register a company with the same name, and then falsely accuse the real owner of cybersquatting so they can take the domain through legal action. *For Cadiz:* a new company calling itself "Cadiz Holdings" could claim that Cadiz's portal domain belongs to it. Cadiz should keep records proving it used the name first.
>
> **(ii) Typosquatting (3)** – registering misspelt versions of a popular domain, with one letter changed or a hyphen added, to catch users who type the address wrongly, e.g. Googgle.com instead of Google.com. *For Cadiz:* squatters may register "cadlz.com" or "cadiz-shop.com" to show fake stores or advertisements to Cadiz's customers. Cadiz should register common misspellings of its domain and monitor new registrations.
>
> **(iii) Name jacking (2)** – registering domain names or social media accounts using the real name of a famous person, to create fake sites. *For Cadiz:* squatters could create fake social media accounts in the name of Cadiz's founder or its celebrity ambassador to mislead customers.
>
> **(iv) Identity theft (2)** – registering a domain that looks identical to a real business's website to pretend to be that business online; users who want the real site land on a phishing site. *For Cadiz:* a look-alike domain could copy Cadiz's portal to steal customers' login and payment details.

**Oct 2024 Q4c** — *Domitri wants to register a trademark for his brand name. Explain the FIVE spectrums of trademarks with a relevant example for each.* **(10 marks)**

> 1. **Generic (2)** – the common name of the product itself; it is the weakest and almost impossible to register. *Example:* naming his product "Encryption Software" (like "Milk" for milk).
> 2. **Descriptive (2)** – directly describes what the product is or does; it is weak and hard to register unless it becomes well known. *Example:* "RealTime Encrypt" (like "Fast Cleaners").
> 3. **Suggestive (2)** – hints at a quality of the product without directly describing it, so customers need some imagination. *Example:* "LockStream", suggesting a secure flow of data (like "Netflix").
> 4. **Arbitrary (2)** – a common word with its own meaning, used for an unrelated product; it is strong. *Example:* "Durian" for encryption software (like "Apple" for computers).
> 5. **Fanciful / coined (2)** – an invented word with no other meaning; it is the strongest and easiest to register and protect. *Example:* "Crytavo" (like "Kodak"). Domitri should choose a fanciful or arbitrary name for the strongest protection.

## 5. 被侵权怎么办？盗版为什么违法？（May 2026 考过）

### ❓ 发现别人抄你的游戏，第一步要做什么？

**大白话**：先**收集证据**（截图、下载记录、你自己创作的草稿和原始码纪录），然后通常是**先寄律师信**要求停止；不停就**上法院告**，要求禁止令和赔偿；也可以请 app store **下架**侵权的 app。

| Action | 中文 | 做什么 |
|---|---|---|
| **Cease-and-desist letter** | 律师警告信 | 要求对方立刻停止使用、下架 |
| **Takedown notice** | 下架通知 | 请 app store、网站主机移除侵权内容 |
| **Legal action** | 提告 | 向法院申请 **injunction**（禁止令）和 **compensation**（赔偿） |

### ✅ 真题满分答案

**May 2026 Q4d** — *Briefly explain TWO actions the developers should take if their IP is infringed.* **(4 marks)**

> 1. **Send a cease-and-desist letter and takedown notices (2)** – through a lawyer, the developers demand that the copying company stop using their game's design and branding immediately, and ask app stores and websites to remove the infringing game.
> 2. **Take legal action (2)** – if the infringement continues, the developers sue for trademark and copyright infringement, asking the court for an injunction to stop the use and for compensation for their losses, using evidence of their original work and registration.

**May 2026 Q4e** — *Explain why downloading pirated digital games is considered an IP violation.* **(3 marks)**

> A game is protected by **copyright**, which gives the owner the exclusive right to copy and distribute it (1). Downloading a pirated copy creates an unauthorised copy without the owner's permission or payment, which infringes that right under laws such as Malaysia's Copyright Act 1987 (1). It takes away income the developers deserve, discourages them from creating new games, and pirated files often contain malware (1).

## 🧾 一页背诵（考前 5 分钟看）

- **IP** = creations of the mind（inventions, art, designs, brand names）· more valuable than physical assets · protects innovation · competitive advantage
- **IPR** = rights to protect & benefit · prevents unauthorised use · 重要性：**exclusive income · prevent use · sue (injunction + compensation) · competitive advantage · encourage innovation**
- **口诀**：做法 → **Patent** · 名字 → **Trademark** · 作品 → **Copyright** · 秘密 → **Trade secret**

- **Copyright**：artistic/literary works · **Copyright Act 1987**（2012, 2022）· **life + 50** · **Berne Convention** · song, novel, code · 自动、不用注册
- **Trade secret**：confidential business info · Coca-Cola · no specific law → contracts & common law · **NDA, access control, employee agreements**
