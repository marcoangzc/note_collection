## 1. What is Ethics? 什么是道德伦理

### ❓ 法律没有规定的事，就可以随便做吗？

不行。社会还有一本"看不见的规则书"，这就是 ethics。

### 🔑 关键词

| English | 中文 | 一句话 |
|---|---|---|
| **Ethics** | 伦理 / 道德规范 | 社会认为可以接受的行为标准 |
| **Computer ethics** | 电脑伦理 | 怎样用电脑才算道德上可以接受 |
| **Software piracy** | 软件盗版 | 没有 license 就复制软件 |

📖 **讲师原句（Revision Notes）**
- "**Ethics = a set of values that define acceptable behaviour in society.**"
- "Ethics helps society decide what is **right or wrong** behaviour."
- "Ethics acts like an **'invisible guidebook'** for behavior **when laws don't cover everything**."
- "**Computer ethics = guidelines for morally acceptable use of computers.**"
- "**Universally wrong**: lying, stealing. What's ethical in one place may be **unethical elsewhere**."

🌰 **讲师的例子**
- **Culture differences**：在马来西亚，没 license 复制 Microsoft Office 是 **unethical and illegal**，但有些国家的人觉得"很正常"。
- **ChatGPT 写功课**：讲师没禁止，算不算 unethical？有人说是（**dishonesty**），有人说不是（**smart use of tools**）。→ 说明 ethics **不一定黑白分明**。

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *Ethics is a set of values that define acceptable behaviour in society; it acts as an "invisible guidebook" when laws do not cover everything. Computer ethics are guidelines for the morally acceptable use of computers.*

## 2. Morals, Ethics, and Laws ⭐⭐⭐（每年必考）

### ❓ "我觉得不对"、"公司规定不能"、"法律禁止"，这三个一样吗？

不一样：
- "我觉得不对" = **Morals**（个人的）
- "公司 / 专业规定不能" = **Ethics**（群体的）
- "法律禁止，会被抓" = **Laws**（政府的）

📖 **讲师的比较表**（考试照这个结构写）

| Aspect | **Morals** 个人道德 | **Ethics** 伦理规范 | **Laws** 法律 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Based on** 根据 | **Religion or personal belief** | **Society/profession's code of conduct** | **Government rules** |
| **Nature** 性质 | **Individual & internal** | **Collective & professional** | **Enforceable by police/courts** |
| **Example** 讲师例子 | Believing **abortion is wrong** | **IT professional code against piracy** | **Abortion legalized** in some countries |
| 违反的后果（补充） | 内疚 | 被公司 / 专业团体处分 | 罚款、坐牢 |

📖 **讲师的对照例子（Contrast）**：*A **lawyer** may **ethically defend a guilty client** (professional duty), though it may seem **immoral** to some.*
→ 意思：符合**专业伦理**，不代表符合每个人的**个人道德**。

### 🌰 IT 例子（自己的例子，考试一定要有）

| | IT 例子 |
|---|---|
| **Morals** | 帮客户修电脑的技术员，**自己觉得**偷看客户私人照片不对，就算没人知道也不看 |
| **Ethics** | 公司的 **code of conduct** 规定：IT staff 只能看工作需要的资料，不能用公司电脑做私人生意 |
| **Laws** | **Computer Crimes Act 1997**：未经授权进入电脑是犯罪；**PDPA 2010**：不能未经同意公开个人资料；**Copyright Act 1987**：盗版违法 |

💡 **加分句**：三者**有重叠但不相等**——例：公司监控员工 email 可能 **legal but unethical**；ethical hacker 没授权就测试银行系统，动机好但 **illegal**。

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *Morals are based on religion or personal belief and are individual and internal; ethics are based on a society's or profession's code of conduct and are collective and professional; laws are government rules enforceable by the police and courts. For example, a lawyer may ethically defend a guilty client, though it may seem immoral to some.*

### 📝 真题
- **Oct 2024 / May 2025 Q1a** — *Discuss the differences among morals, ethics and law, with an IT example for each.* (9)
  ✅ 每个 term 3 分 = **Based on (1) + Nature (1) + IT 例子 (1)**。最后加一句"三者重叠但不相等"。
- **Oct 2025 Q1a** — *Provide a scenario related to IT to explain the differences.* (9)
  ✅ 用**一个故事**串三个：*Daniel, a database administrator at an online shop, is told by his manager to secretly copy customer records for a marketing partner.* → **Morals**：他自己觉得背叛客户是不对的 · **Ethics**：公司 code of conduct 要他保护资料 · **Law**：**PDPA 2010** 禁止未经同意把个人资料给第三方，会被罚款。
- **Jan 2025 Q1a** — *Distinguish morals and ethics in IT, with an example each.* (8) → 用比较表的 Based on、Nature、违反后果 + 两个 IT 例子。
- **May 2026 Q1b** — *Describe morals, ethics and laws in computing.* (6) → 每个 2 分 = 定义 + computing 例子。

## 3. 5 Steps of Ethical Decision-Making 道德决策五步（tips 没写，但考过）

### ❓ 老板叫你把客户资料交给别人，你会怎么"一步一步"决定要不要做？

```
 1. Develop problem statement      写清楚问题是什么
        ↓
 2. Identify alternatives          想出几个方案
        ↓
 3. Evaluate & choose alternative  比较，选最好的
        ↓
 4. Implement decision             去执行
        ↓
 5. Evaluate results               看结果 ──→ 不成功？回到 1
```

| Step | 中文 | 🌰 例子（老板要客户资料） |
|---|---|---|
| 1 **Develop problem statement** | 写问题 | "老板要我把客户资料给营销公司，客户没有同意" |
| 2 **Identify alternatives** | 列方案 | 照做 / 拒绝 / 先问法律部门、取得客户同意 |
| 3 **Evaluate & choose alternative** | 比较与选择 | 照做违法 → 选"先取得同意" |
| 4 **Implement decision** | 执行 | email 老板和法律部门，建立 consent 流程 |
| 5 **Evaluate results** | 检讨结果 | 一个月后看有没有投诉；不成功就回到第 1 步 |

📝 **Oct 2024 Q1b** — *Describe the FIVE major steps involved in ethical decision-making.* (5) → 1 步 1 分，步骤名 + 一句做什么。

## 4. Why Ethics in IT? 为什么 IT 特别需要伦理

### ❓ 为什么 IT 行业的人特别常碰到道德问题？

因为 IT 什么地方都碰到：政府、工作、私人生活。

📖 **讲师原句**
- "IT affects **governance, work, personal life** → ethical decisions matter."
- "In IT, ethical decisions often involve **balancing company interests with individual rights**."

| Issue | 中文 | 讲师举的例子 |
|---|---|---|
| **Privacy** | 隐私 | **employee monitoring, email usage** |
| **Data security** | 资料安全 | protection against **manipulation / leaks** |
| **Global concerns** | 全球问题 | **cross-border data, cybercrime** |

🌰 讲师例子：公司**追踪员工的浏览记录**防止滥用；公司**装软件记录员工的键盘输入 (keystrokes)** → 公司利益 vs 员工隐私，这就是 ethical issue。

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *Ethics matters in IT because IT affects governance, work and personal life, and IT decisions often involve balancing company interests with individual rights, for example in privacy (employee monitoring), data security (protection against leaks) and global concerns (cross-border data and cybercrime).*

## 5. Ethics for IT Professionals IT 专业人员的伦理

### ❓ 为什么医生、律师要守专业守则？IT 人员也要吗？

要。因为别人**把最重要的资料交给你**。

📖 **讲师原句**：*Who is an IT professional?* "**Anyone working in IT** (e.g. developers, engineers, analysts)."

**Professional 的 4 个特征（Characteristics of professionals）**

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| **Advanced knowledge & creativity** | 高深的知识与创造力 |
| **Independent judgment** | 能独立判断 |
| **Commitment to lifelong learning** | 终身学习 |
| **Contribution to society** | 对社会有贡献 |

**Professional Code of Ethics（专业守则）**
- Sets out **goals + rules** for members.
- Guides behaviour **beyond just following laws**（比法律要求更高）。

🌰 讲师例子：**IEEE** 和 **ACM** 有专业守则；**医生信任 IT 人员**会把病历保密——IT staff 泄露病历，**public trust is lost**。

## 6. Common Ethical Issues for IT Users ⭐（May 2026 考 9 分）

### ❓ 普通员工用公司电脑，最常犯哪三种道德问题？

| Issue | 中文 | 📖 讲师定义 | 🌰 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Software Piracy** | 软件盗版 | **Illegal copying of software** | 一个 Office license 装在 30 台电脑 |
| **Inappropriate Use of IT** | 不当使用 IT | **Browsing, gaming, sharing offensive content at work, wasting company resources** | 上班时间用公司网络看电影、玩游戏 |
| **Inappropriate Sharing of Information** | 不当分享资讯 | **Leaking confidential business or customer data** | 员工**不小心**把客户的信用卡号分享出去 → **breach of privacy** |

🌰 讲师例子：一个电商网站的**信用卡资料库外泄** → 顾客**可能再也不在那里网购**。

> 💬 **答题句 (EN)** — *Three common ethical issues for IT users are software piracy (illegal copying of software), inappropriate use of IT (browsing, gaming or sharing offensive content at work, wasting company resources), and inappropriate sharing of information (leaking confidential business or customer data).*

### 📝 真题：May 2026 Q1a — *Explain THREE common ethical issues faced by IT users.* (9 marks)
✅ 每个 3 分 = **讲师定义 (1) + 为什么是问题 / 后果 (1) + 具体例子 (1)**：
1. **Software piracy** – illegal copying of software. It takes income away from developers and exposes the company to lawsuits and malware. *e.g.* an IT officer installs one licensed copy of Microsoft Office on 30 office PCs.
2. **Inappropriate use of IT** – browsing, gaming or sharing offensive content at work, wasting company resources. It reduces productivity and offensive content can create a hostile workplace. *e.g.* an employee streams movies on the office network during working hours.
3. **Inappropriate sharing of information** – leaking confidential business or customer data. It breaches customers' privacy and may break the PDPA 2010. *e.g.* an employee accidentally emails a spreadsheet of customer credit card numbers to the wrong person.

## 7. Certifications in IT IT 认证

### ❓ 雇主怎么知道你真的会？

看你的**证书 (certification)**。

📖 **讲师原句**：*"**Proof of skills**, often **boosts career & salary**."*

| | **Vendor Certifications** 厂商认证 | **Industry Association Certifications** 行业协会认证 |
|---|---|---|
| 📖 讲师 | **Specific to a company's products** | **Broader**, require **exams & ongoing learning** |
| 例子 | **Cisco, Microsoft, AWS** | **(ISC)², ISACA** |
| 🌰 | AWS Cloud Certification = 会管理云端服务 | CISSP（由 (ISC)² 颁发）= 资安专业 |

🌰 讲师例子：*A **Cisco-certified** network engineer might be **hired faster** because certification **reassures employers** of basic competence.*
