## 1. IP 与 IPR 为什么重要 ⭐

**核心点**：IP = **creations of the mind**（发明、艺术、设计、品牌名称）；常常**比实体资产更值钱**；保护创新、带来**竞争优势**。IPR = 给创作者**保护并从作品获益**的权利，**防止未经授权使用**。

**写"为什么重要"的四个角度**（每个都要连到 case 的具体东西）：
1. **Exclusive right to earn income**（独家获利、授权收费）
2. **Stop unauthorised copying**（阻止抄袭）
3. **Legal remedy**（可以告、拿禁令、要赔偿）
4. **Competitive advantage / brand value / investor confidence**（差异化、品牌、吸引投资）

### ✅ 高分答法 — Oct 2024 Q4a: *Examine why IPR are important to Domitri. Provide relevant justifications.* (5 marks)
1. **Control over who uses his algorithm** – Domitri spent years creating a real-time encryption algorithm now used widely by technology companies. IPR give him the exclusive legal right to decide who may use it, so when a Penang software firm wants to build it into its banking app, it must first ask his permission (1–2).
2. **Income from licensing** – he can license the algorithm, e.g. charging each company an RM50,000 yearly licence or a royalty per installation, turning his invention into a steady income instead of letting others profit for free (1).
3. **Legal protection against misuse** – if a company copies the algorithm into its product without permission, Domitri can take legal action for an injunction to stop sales and claim damages (1).
4. **Reputation and competitive advantage** – protected ownership shows the algorithm is his proprietary creation, strengthening his reputation as a leading Malaysian developer and making investors and partners more willing to work with him (1).

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2025 Q4a: *Examine why IPR are important to Nintendo. Provide relevant examples.* (5 marks)
1. **Protects its most valuable assets** – Nintendo's characters (Mario, Pikachu, Link), game code and brand are worth far more than its offices and factories; IPR ensure only Nintendo and its licensees can use them (1–2).
2. **Exclusive income from games and merchandise** – *e.g.* a toy company in Malaysia that wants to sell Super Mario plush toys must pay Nintendo a licence fee, generating revenue from every product sold (1).
3. **Stops counterfeits and piracy** – *e.g.* Nintendo can demand the removal of websites offering pirated Nintendo Switch games and seize fake Pokémon cards sold at night markets (1).
4. **Keeps customer trust and brand quality** – because unofficial products cannot use the Nintendo name or logo, buyers know an item with the logo meets Nintendo's quality standards; *e.g.* in 2024 Nintendo filed a patent lawsuit against the maker of *Palworld*, showing how actively it defends its IP (1).

### ✅ 高分答法 — Jan 2025 Q4a: *Determine the importance of protecting IPR for Cadiz Sdn. Bhd. Discuss how IPR contributes to competitive advantage.* (5 marks)
- **Importance (2–3):** Cadiz's AI-powered shopping portal — its recommendation engine, unique page design and branding — cost significant money and months of development. Without adequate IP protection, a competitor was able to copy its design features and branding right after launch. Proper IPR (registered trademark, copyright in the portal's code and design, and trade-secret controls on the AI model) give Cadiz the legal right to stop the competitor and claim compensation.
- **Competitive advantage (2–3):** IPR make Cadiz's innovations exclusive: if only Cadiz can use its look, name and AI features, customers associate the unique shopping experience with Cadiz alone rather than with the copycat. This protects Cadiz's market share and brand reputation, allows it to earn licensing income (e.g. licensing its AI engine to other retailers), and makes it more attractive to investors because its key assets are legally secured.

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2026 Q4a: *Explain TWO reasons why Intellectual Property Rights are important for the game creators.* (6 marks)
1. **They secure the income from the creators' own work** (3) – the young developers created original characters, music, logos and gameplay. With IPR they alone can sell the game, merchandise and soundtrack, or license them. *E.g.* when the game went viral with 500,000 downloads, a toy company offered to make figures of the main character; with registered rights the team can demand a royalty on every figure instead of seeing copycats profit.
2. **They let the creators stop copying and protect their brand** (3) – other companies are already copying the design and branding. IPR give the developers the legal right to demand the removal of copy games from app stores and to sue for damages, so players are not confused by look-alike games and the team's reputation and future sales are protected.

## 2. 四种 IP（每一种要说"保护什么"）

| Type | 保护什么 | 条件 / 期限（照讲师 tips） | 怎么取得 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Patent** | **发明**（技术方法、装置的功能） | **New、inventive、industrially applicable**；Patents Act 1983；**15 years from grant**（现行法：申请日起 20 年） | 向 **MyIPO** 申请，要公开发明内容 |
| **Trademark** | 区分商品 / 服务的**名称、logo、口号** | Trademarks Act 2019；可每 10 年续期（*extra*） | 向 MyIPO 注册，指定类别（例：Class 9 软件、Class 41 娱乐） |
| **Copyright** | **表达**：程序码、美术、音乐、文字、角色设计（**不保护 idea 本身**） | Copyright Act 1987（2012、2022 修订）；**作者终身 + 50 年**；Berne Convention | **创作完成就自动拥有**，不必注册（保留创作纪录当证据） |
| **Trade secret** | 有商业价值的**机密资讯**（配方、演算法细节、客户名单） | 马来西亚**没有专门法律**，靠**合约与 common law** | NDA、access control、员工合约；**只要保密就一直有效**（Coca-Cola 配方） |

### ✅ 高分答法 — Oct 2024 Q4b: *Suggest to Domitri the TWO most suitable types of IPR for his encryption algorithm. Justify.* (10 marks)

1. **Patent** (5) – a patent protects a new, inventive and industrially applicable invention, giving the owner the exclusive right to use, sell and license it for a fixed period (15 years from grant under the Patents Act 1983, as in the notes). *Justification:* Domitri's real-time encryption **method**, as implemented in software systems, is innovative and widely used; a patent protects the **functional idea itself**, so no company can use the same method — even if it writes completely different code — without his licence. This matches his wish to permit or restrict usage, e.g. granting a licence to a Penang bank-software firm for RM50,000 a year while refusing a competitor. He should file with MyIPO before publicly publishing the technique. *(Note: a pure mathematical formula alone may not be patentable, so the claim should cover its technical application.)*
2. **Copyright** (5) – copyright automatically protects the original **expression** of his work — the source code, object code and documentation of the software — for his lifetime plus 50 years, with no registration needed. *Justification:* even if someone does not use his patented method, they cannot copy or redistribute his actual software. If a former client copies the compiled library into its own product, Domitri can sue for copyright infringement. He should keep dated records of his code (e.g. Git history) as evidence of authorship.

*（另一组可接受：**Trade secret + copyright**——把演算法细节保密，只在 NDA 下授权；但 patent 需要公开，所以 trade secret 和 patent 只能选一个路线，写的时候说明这点会加分。）*

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2025 Q4b: *Suggest TWO most suitable types of IPR that Nintendo should consider for their market content. Justify.* (10 marks)

1. **Trademark** (5) – protects names, logos and signs that distinguish Nintendo's goods from others. *Justification:* Nintendo's market value depends on names and logos such as "Nintendo", "Nintendo Switch", "Super Mario" and "Pokémon". Registering them (e.g. for games, consoles and toys) lets Nintendo stop anyone selling "Super Mario" T-shirts or a phone game called "Pokemon Go Adventure" that confuses customers. Trademarks can be renewed indefinitely, so the brand stays protected as long as Nintendo uses it — ideal for characters that have sold for decades.
2. **Copyright** (5) – protects original creative expression: game code, character artwork, animation, music and stories. *Justification:* Nintendo's games and character designs are its core content; copyright automatically stops others from copying the Super Mario artwork, the Zelda soundtrack or the game software itself. It is the basis for removing pirated Nintendo Switch game files from websites and for acting against fan-made games that copy its levels and characters.

## 3. Case：Cadiz、Disney vs Edward、indie game

### ✅ 高分答法 — Jan 2025 Q4b: *Interpret each type of IP protection relevant to Cadiz and how each could protect the AI-powered shopping portal.* (3 + 3 + 2 + 2 marks)

- **(i) Patents (3)** – a patent protects a new, inventive and industrially applicable invention for a fixed period. Cadiz could patent the **technical method** behind its AI feature — e.g. a new way of generating product recommendations from a customer's uploaded photo — if it is genuinely new. Competitors would then be unable to build the same feature even with their own code, and Cadiz could license it to other retailers.
- **(ii) Copyrights (3)** – copyright automatically protects original expression. It covers Cadiz's **source code, page layouts, graphics, icons, product photos and written content**. When the competitor copied the portal's unique design elements, Cadiz could claim copyright infringement, supported by its dated design files and code repository.
- **(iii) Trademarks (2)** – a trademark protects the name, logo and slogan that identify Cadiz. Cadiz should register "Cadiz" and its logo with MyIPO for online retail services, so the competitor's use of similar branding can be stopped and customers are not confused.
- **(iv) Trade secrets (2)** – confidential business information protected by keeping it secret. Cadiz should protect its AI model's training data, tuning parameters and supplier pricing through NDAs with its 30 developers, access control and employment contracts; this protection lasts as long as the information stays secret.

### ✅ 高分答法 — Oct 2025 Q4a: *Explain how the Walt Disney Company would apply its IPR to protect its contributions throughout the production and distribution process. Provide a specific example for each.* (5 marks)
- **Production – trade secrets and copyright:** during production Disney keeps scripts, character designs and plot twists secret under NDAs with animators and voice actors (*e.g.* staff working on an unreleased film cannot share concept art online); every drawing, 3D model and piece of music becomes a copyright work the moment it is created (2).
- **Distribution – copyright licensing:** Disney licenses its films to cinemas and streaming platforms under contracts that control where and how long they are shown; *e.g.* a Malaysian cinema chain may screen a film only during the licensed period, and copying it is infringement (1–2).
- **Merchandising – trademarks:** the "Disney" name, logos and character names used on products are registered as trademarks for toys, clothing and apps, so only licensed manufacturers can sell official merchandise; *e.g.* a Malaysian shop selling unlicensed Snow White dolls with the Disney logo can be stopped (1).
- **Technology – patents:** Disney can patent new animation or theme-park technologies it invents, stopping competitors from copying them (1).

### ✅ 高分答法 — Oct 2025 Q4b: *If Disney took legal action against Edward, describe how it could use TWO relevant types of IPR to address the infringement. For each, provide ONE concrete action or claim.* (10 marks)

⚠️ **高分关键句**：*Snow White* 与七个小矮人来自格林童话，**"一个小矮人角色"这个 idea 本身不受保护**；Disney 能主张的是**它自己画出来的独特设计**（脸、服装、颜色、名字的呈现）。Edward 的角色"closely resembles"其中一个，所以侵犯的是 Disney 的 **expression**。

1. **Copyright** (5) – Disney owns the copyright in its own artistic design of the Seven Dwarfs — their specific faces, costumes, colours and animation style — as drawn in its film and later works. Edward's dwarf "closely resembles" one of them, so it is likely a copy or substantial reproduction of that artwork, used in XYZ's application without permission. *Concrete action:* Disney's lawyers send Edward and XYZ a **cease-and-desist letter** demanding that the character be removed from the app within 14 days; if they refuse, Disney files a copyright infringement suit seeking an **injunction** to stop distribution and **damages** or an account of profits earned from the app.
2. **Trademark** (5) – Disney registers trademarks for its brand and for character names and images it uses on products such as apps and merchandise (assume the dwarf character is among them). If XYZ's app uses the look-alike dwarf as its mascot, users may believe the app is made or endorsed by Disney, which is trademark infringement (and passing off). *Concrete action:* Disney files a **trademark infringement complaint** asking the court to order XYZ to stop using the character in the app and its marketing, and asks the app stores to **take down** the app until the character is replaced.
- *Lesson for Edward:* he could have avoided this by designing an original character from the public-domain idea of a dwarf, with his own look and name.

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2026 Q4c: *Describe THREE types of Intellectual Property Rights that the developers can use to protect their game.* (6 marks)
Use the team's actual assets (2 each):
1. **Copyright** – automatically protects the game's code, original characters' artwork, music and story; *e.g.* if another studio copies the hero character's design or rips the soundtrack, the team can demand removal and damages.
2. **Trademark** – register the game's name and logo (e.g. "Hikayat Hutan") with MyIPO for games and merchandise, so no other company can release "Hikayat Hutan 2" or sell T-shirts with the logo.
3. **Patent** – if one of the unique gameplay features is a genuinely new technical invention (e.g. a new method of syncing multiplayer moves over weak mobile networks), the team can patent it so competitors cannot copy the mechanism itself for 15 years from grant.
*(Trade secret is also acceptable: keeping the source code and the unreleased level designs confidential under NDAs.)*

## 4. Trademark Spectrum（五级）⭐

从最弱到最强：

| Level | 意思 | Tips 例 | 📈 你自己的例子（软件 / 加密品牌） |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Generic** | 就是商品的普通名称——**不能注册** | "Milk" | "Encryption Software" |
| **Descriptive** | **直接描述**商品特点——很弱，要长期使用建立 secondary meaning | "Fast Cleaners" | "FastEncrypt" |
| **Suggestive** | **暗示**特点，要想一下才懂——可注册 | "Netflix" | "LockStream"（暗示安全的资料流） |
| **Arbitrary** | 真实的字，但**和商品无关**——强 | "Apple"（电脑） | "Kancil"（鼷鹿）用在加密软件 |
| **Fanciful / coined** | **自己发明的新字**——**最强** | "Kodak" | "Zyphora" |

### ✅ 高分答法 — Oct 2024 Q4c: *Domitri would like to register a trademark for his brand name. Explain the FIVE spectrums of trademarks with a relevant example for each.* (10 marks)

2 分一级（意思 + Domitri 的例子与建议）：
1. **Generic** – the common name of the product itself; it can never be registered because everyone must be free to use it. *E.g.* "Encryption Software" — Domitri cannot own this name.
2. **Descriptive** – directly describes a quality or function of the product; weak and registrable only after long use makes customers link it to one company. *E.g.* "SecureFast Encryption" simply describes the software, so competitors could argue they also need these words.
3. **Suggestive** – hints at a quality but needs imagination to connect; distinctive enough to register. *E.g.* "CipherWave" suggests smooth, secure data flow without directly describing it.
4. **Arbitrary** – a real word with no connection to the product; strongly protected. *E.g.* "Kancil" (mousedeer) for encryption software — a familiar Malaysian word, memorable and unrelated to encryption.
5. **Fanciful (coined)** – an invented word with no meaning except as the brand; the strongest and easiest to register and defend. *E.g.* "Domyptix", made up from his name — no one else can claim it.
- **Recommendation:** Domitri should choose a fanciful or arbitrary name and register it with MyIPO under Class 9 (software), because these give the widest and strongest protection.

## 5. IP Issues ⭐（每个配一个自己的情境）

| Issue | 核心点 | 📈 你的情境 |
|---|---|---|
| **Plagiarism** | 抄别人的点子或文字当成自己的 | 学生把网上一篇 1,500 字的文章改几个字交作业；developer 从 GitHub 复制整个模组、删掉原作者名字 |
| **Reverse engineering** | 拆解产品去研究设计 / 技术；某些情况合法，但有道德争议 | 竞争对手反编译 Studio Rimba 的 APK，找出关卡生成的方法，做出几乎一样的游戏 |
| **Open vs closed source** | Open（如 Linux）可自由使用修改，**但要遵守 license**；closed（如 Windows）是专有的 | 公司把 GPL 授权的开源程序码放进自己的付费 app，却不公开修改后的程序码——违反 license |
| **Competitive intelligence (CI)** | 收集竞争对手的专利与市场资料；合法来源 OK，**不当取得机密**就是问题 | 用公开专利资料 OK；但挖角对手员工并要他带走客户名单就越界 |
| **Trademark infringement** | 未经授权使用别人的商标，造成混淆 | 夜市卖的 "Adidas" 鞋；或 app 取名 "KedaiKu Mart" 骗用户以为是 KedaiKu |
| **Cybersquatting** | 注册和知名品牌相同 / 相似的网域来获利或诈骗 | 有人抢先注册 "hikayathutan.com"，开价 RM30,000 卖回给开发团队 |

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2025 Q4c / Oct 2025 Q4c: *Briefly explain FIVE issues related to intellectual property protection.* (10 marks)

2 分一个（意思 1 + 你的具体情境 1）：
1. **Plagiarism** – presenting someone else's words, ideas or code as one's own without credit. *E.g.* a developer at a Kuala Lumpur agency copies a 300-line payment module from a GitHub project, deletes the original author's name and sells it to a client as his own work.
2. **Reverse engineering** – taking apart a product or software to learn its design and technology, sometimes to copy it; it may be legal in some contexts but is ethically debated. *E.g.* a competitor decompiles Studio Rimba's game APK, studies how its levels are generated and releases a near-identical game three months later.
3. **Open-source vs closed-source licensing** – open-source code may be used and modified freely only under its licence terms, while closed-source software is proprietary. *E.g.* a start-up puts GPL-licensed code into its paid mobile app but refuses to release its modified source code, breaching the licence; meanwhile an employee copies Microsoft's closed-source code, which is plainly prohibited.
4. **Competitive intelligence** – gathering and analysing information about competitors' products, patents and markets; legitimate from public sources but an IP problem when confidential information is obtained improperly. *E.g.* KedaiKu legitimately studies a rival's published patents, but crosses the line when it hires the rival's manager on condition he brings the supplier price list.
5. **Trademark infringement** – using another's trademark without permission in a way that confuses customers about the source of goods. *E.g.* a seller on an online marketplace lists "Nike Air" shoes that are counterfeits, misleading buyers and damaging Nike's brand; Nike can demand removal and sue.
6. **Cybersquatting** – registering a domain name identical or similar to a famous brand in bad faith to profit from it. *E.g.* after *Hikayat Hutan* goes viral, someone registers "hikayathutan.com" and offers to sell it to the developers for RM30,000, or uses it to show ads.

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2026 Q4b: *Explain TWO intellectual property issues that may occur if the developers fail to protect their game.* (6 marks)
1. **Trademark infringement / copycat branding** (3) – without a registered trademark, other companies can release games with similar names and logos, e.g. "Hikayat Hutan: Legends" with a nearly identical logo. Players download the copy by mistake, the original team loses downloads and in-app purchase income, and bad reviews of the copy hurt the original's reputation.
2. **Copying of game design and assets (copyright infringement / reverse engineering)** (3) – competitors can extract the characters, music and code from the app file and reuse them, e.g. a studio rips the soundtrack and hero sprite into its own game within weeks. Without records of authorship and quick action, the developers find it harder to prove ownership and stop the copying.

*（Cybersquatting 也可以：有人抢注游戏名称的网域或社群帐号。）*

### ✅ 高分答法 — Jan 2025 Q4c: *Explain to Cadiz the types of cybersquatting: (i) reverse cybersquatting, (ii) typosquatting, (iii) name jacking, (iv) identity theft.* (3 + 3 + 2 + 2 marks)

- **(i) Reverse cybersquatting (3)** – a party abuses domain-dispute rules to take a legitimately registered domain from its rightful owner by falsely accusing the owner of cybersquatting. *E.g.* a newly formed "Cadiz Trading" company files a complaint claiming Cadiz's portal domain infringes its name and tries to seize it; Cadiz should keep dated records proving it registered and used the domain first.
- **(ii) Typosquatting (3)** – registering misspelled versions of a popular domain to catch users who type the address wrongly. *E.g.* someone registers "cadlz.com" and "cadiz-shop.com" to show a fake store that steals login details; Cadiz should buy common misspellings and redirect them to its real site.
- **(iii) Name jacking (2)** – registering domains or social media accounts using a real person's name. *E.g.* a fake Instagram account in the name of Cadiz's founder announces a fake "90% sale" to lure customers to a scam site.
- **(iv) Identity theft (2)** – registering a look-alike domain to impersonate the business online. *E.g.* "cadiz-my.com" copies the portal's design exactly and collects customers' card details; Cadiz should monitor new registrations and report such domains quickly.

## 6. 被侵权时怎么办 & 盗版

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2026 Q4d: *Briefly explain TWO actions the developers should take when their intellectual property is infringed.* (4 marks)
1. **Send a cease-and-desist letter and platform takedown requests** (2) – e.g. the team's lawyer writes to the copycat studio demanding it remove the copied characters within 14 days, and files takedown reports with Google Play and the App Store with evidence (original design files dated before the copy's release), so the copy is removed quickly.
2. **Take legal action** (2) – if the infringer continues, sue for copyright or trademark infringement in court to obtain an injunction stopping sales and damages for lost income; for counterfeit merchandise, report to the enforcement authorities (e.g. KPDN) for raids and seizure.

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2026 Q4e: *Explain why downloading pirated digital games is considered an intellectual property violation.* (3 marks)
The copyright owner has the exclusive right to reproduce and distribute the game, and players get a lawful copy only by buying a licence (1). Downloading a pirated copy creates an unauthorised reproduction, which infringes copyright under the Copyright Act 1987 (1). *E.g.* if 50,000 players download *Hikayat Hutan* (priced at RM25) from a piracy site, the four developers lose about RM1.25 million in potential sales that fund updates and salaries; pirated files may also contain malware (1).

## 7. 自己练习的新情境题（附高分答案）

**P1. Kopi Kaki wants to protect its secret cold-brew recipe and its cup logo. Which IPR for each? Justify. (4 marks)**
> *Recipe – trade secret:* it gives a business advantage only while secret; Kopi Kaki should share it only with 3 senior baristas under NDAs and store it in a locked file, and protection lasts as long as it stays secret (like Coca-Cola's formula). A patent would force it to publish the recipe. *Logo – trademark:* register the cup logo with MyIPO for café services, so another café cannot use a look-alike cup that confuses customers.

**P2. A student uses ChatGPT to write a whole assignment and submits it as her own. Is this an IP issue? Explain. (3 marks)**
> It is plagiarism: she presents work she did not create as her own, misleading the lecturer about her ability. Even if the AI text is not someone's copyrighted work, it breaks academic integrity rules; if the lecturer did not allow AI, it is also dishonest under the university's code of conduct.

**P3. Explain why copyright does not stop another developer from making a game with the same idea. (2 marks)**
> Copyright protects the expression, not the idea. Another studio may make a jungle-adventure game with a mousedeer hero, but it cannot copy Studio Rimba's actual code, character artwork, music or level designs.

## 8. Cheat sheet

- **IP** = creations of the mind；比实体资产更值钱；**IPR** = 保护并获益、阻止未授权使用。
- **重要性四角度**：独家收入 · 阻止抄袭 · 法律补救 · 竞争优势 / 品牌 / 投资人信心。
- **Patent**：new + inventive + industrially applicable；Patents Act 1983；15 years from grant（现行 20 年自申请日）；保护**功能 / 方法**。
- **Trademark**：Trademarks Act 2019；名称、logo；spectrum：generic < descriptive < suggestive < arbitrary < fanciful。
- **Copyright**：Copyright Act 1987（2012、2022 修订）；life + 50；自动取得；保护**表达**不保护 idea。
- **Trade secret**：没有专门法律；NDA、access control、合约；保密就一直有效。
- **Issues**：plagiarism · reverse engineering · open vs closed source · competitive intelligence · trademark infringement · cybersquatting（reverse、typosquatting、name jacking、identity theft）。
- **被侵权**：cease-and-desist + takedown → 告（injunction + damages）→ 报执法单位。
- **Case 答法**：说出**保护的是哪一样东西** + **具体行动**（向 MyIPO 注册什么、发什么信、向哪个平台下架）。
