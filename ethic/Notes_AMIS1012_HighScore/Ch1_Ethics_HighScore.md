## 1. What is Ethics

**核心点**：ethics = 社会认为可以接受的行为的一套价值观；法律没管到的地方，它像一本"看不见的指南"；computer ethics = 使用电脑时道德上可接受的准则；**同一件事在不同文化可能被看成不同**（撒谎、偷窃普遍被认为错；盗版在某些地方被容忍）。

**📈 高分情境（文化差异）**
> In Malaysia, installing an unlicensed copy of Adobe Photoshop on an office PC is both unethical and illegal under the Copyright Act 1987. However, Jason, a freelance designer who grew up where cracked software was sold openly at night markets, may see it as "normal" because everyone around him did it. This shows that ethics depends on the society's shared values, which is why companies such as KedaiKu Sdn Bhd write a clear software policy for all staff instead of relying on each person's upbringing.

## 2. Morals vs Ethics vs Law ⭐⭐

| | Morals | Ethics | Law |
|---|---|---|---|
| 来源 | 个人信念（宗教、文化、家庭） | 社会 / 行业 / 公司的行为准则 | 政府 |
| 范围 | 个人、内在 | 群体、专业 | 全国 |
| 形式 | 不成文 | 通常成文（code of conduct、company policy） | 成文法 |
| 违反后果 | 良心不安 | 纪律处分、被开除、失去认证 | 罚款、坐牢、民事赔偿 |

**关系**：三者重叠但不相等——**合法但不道德**（例如在隐私政策的小字里偷偷卖顾客资料）、**合乎个人道德但违法**（例如为了揭发贪污而入侵系统）。

### ✅ 高分答法 — Oct 2025 Q1a: *Provide a scenario related to IT to explain the differences among morals, ethics, and law.* (9 marks)

用**一个情境**串起三个概念，每个概念 3 分（定义 + 情境中的表现 + 后果）：

> **Scenario:** Aina is a junior IT executive at KedaiKu Sdn Bhd, an online grocery platform in Petaling Jaya with 50,000 registered customers. On a Friday evening her manager asks her to export the customer list — names, phone numbers and home addresses — and pass it to a friend's insurance agency, which will pay KedaiKu RM5,000 for the data.
>
> **Morals (3):** Morals are an individual's personal beliefs about right and wrong, shaped by religion, culture and upbringing, and they differ from person to person. Aina was taught by her parents that using people's information without asking is dishonest, so she feels uncomfortable even though nobody would find out. Her colleague Farid, however, thinks "everyone sells data," so his conscience does not stop him. Breaking one's morals only leads to guilt, not punishment.
>
> **Ethics (3):** Ethics are the code of conduct expected by a society, profession or organisation, usually written down. KedaiKu's IT code of conduct states that customer data may be used only for processing orders, and professional codes such as the ACM Code of Ethics require IT staff to respect privacy and honour confidentiality. If Aina exports the list she breaches professional ethics and can face a written warning or dismissal, even if no court is involved.
>
> **Law (3):** Law is a set of rules made and enforced by the government, with penalties. Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, personal data cannot be disclosed to a third party without the customer's consent. If the 50,000 records are shared, KedaiKu and the people involved can be fined and even imprisoned, and customers can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.
>
> **Difference:** here all three point the same way, but they are not identical — if the customers had "agreed" through a hidden clause in a 20-page privacy policy, the sharing might be legal, yet it would still be unethical and against Aina's morals.

### ✅ 高分答法 — Oct 2024 Q1a / May 2025 Q1a: *Discuss the differences among morals, ethics and law. Provide an example (related to IT) for each term.* (9 marks)

三个 term 各 3 分（定义 + 特点 + **自己的 IT 例子**）：

1. **Morals** – an individual's own beliefs about what is right and wrong, shaped by religion, culture and family; they are unwritten, differ from person to person and are enforced only by one's conscience. *Example:* Kumar, a technician at a phone repair shop in Ipoh, is fixing a customer's cracked iPhone and sees the photo gallery unlocked. Nobody would know if he scrolled through the photos, but he closes the app because his personal values tell him it is wrong to invade someone's privacy.
2. **Ethics** – the standards of behaviour expected by a society, profession or organisation, usually written in a code of conduct and enforced by the group through disciplinary action. *Example:* MedikaCare's IT policy forbids staff from looking up records of patients they are not treating. When a clerk searched her ex-boyfriend's medical record out of curiosity, the audit log flagged the access and she received a final warning — no law suit was needed, but the professional rule was broken.
3. **Law** – rules created by the government that state what people may or may not do, enforced by police and courts with fines or imprisonment. *Example:* A former employee of KedaiKu used his old password to log into the company server a week after resigning and deleted 2,000 product listings. This is unauthorised access and modification under the Computer Crimes Act 1997, so KedaiKu reported him to the police.
- **Overall difference:** morals are personal, ethics are collective and professional, and law is enforced by the state; an act can be legal but unethical (tracking employees' keystrokes without telling them) or ethical but illegal.

### ✅ 高分答法 — Jan 2025 Q1a: *Distinguish between morals and ethics in the context of IT, with an example of each.* (8 marks)

先写比较（4 分），再写两个具体例子（各 2 分）：

| | Morals | Ethics |
|---|---|---|
| Source (1) | Individual conscience, religion, culture, upbringing | A society, organisation or profession |
| Scope (1) | Personal – differs from person to person | Shared – applies equally to all members of the group |
| Form (1) | Unwritten | Usually written (code of ethics, IT policy) |
| Enforcement (1) | Self-enforced through guilt | Enforced by the group (warning, dismissal, loss of certification) |

- **Morals example (2):** Wei Ling, a mobile developer at a start-up in Penang, is asked to build a "spin-the-wheel" feature that encourages teenagers to buy RM2 loot boxes. The company and the law allow it, but she refuses because her personal beliefs tell her it is like gambling. Her decision comes only from her own conscience.
- **Ethics example (2):** Farid holds an ISC2 CISSP certification. During a penetration test for a bank, he finds that the bank's mobile app leaks account balances. The (ISC)² Code of Ethics requires him to report it only to the client and not disclose it to others; if he posted it on social media, (ISC)² could revoke his certification, even though his personal morals might see it as "warning the public."

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2026 Q1b: *Describe morals, ethics, and laws in computing.* (6 marks)
每个 2 分 = 定义 1 句 + 具体 computing 例子 1 句。可直接用上面三个例子的**缩短版**：
- **Morals** – personal beliefs about right and wrong. *E.g.* Kumar does not browse a customer's photos while repairing her phone because he believes it is wrong.
- **Ethics** – written codes of conduct of a profession or organisation. *E.g.* MedikaCare's policy forbids staff from opening records of patients they are not treating; a clerk who did so received a final warning.
- **Laws** – government rules with legal penalties. *E.g.* a former KedaiKu employee who logged in after resigning and deleted 2,000 listings committed an offence under the Computer Crimes Act 1997.

## 3. Why Ethics in IT

**核心点**：IT 影响政府、工作、个人生活每个角落；IT 的道德决策常常要**平衡公司利益和个人权利**；议题包括 **privacy（员工监控、email）**、**data security（资料被操纵 / 外泄）**、**global concerns（跨国资料、cybercrime）**。

**📈 高分情境（公司利益 vs 个人权利）**
> KedaiKu installs keystroke-logging software on all 120 office PCs to catch staff who leak supplier prices. It helps the company, but it also records employees' personal banking passwords typed during lunch breaks. The ethical approach is to tell staff in writing what is monitored, limit monitoring to work systems, and never store passwords — balancing the company's need for security with employees' right to privacy.

## 4. Ethics for IT Professionals

**核心点**：IT professional = 在 IT 领域工作的人（developer、engineer、analyst…）。Professional 的特征：**advanced knowledge & creativity、independent judgment、commitment to lifelong learning、contribution to society**。**Professional code of ethics** 列出目标与规则，引导超越法律要求的行为（例：ACM、IEEE）。

**📈 高分情境（为什么需要 code of ethics）**
> Doctors at MedikaCare trust the hospital's IT team to keep 80,000 patient records confidential. When a vendor offers Farid, the system administrator, RM10,000 for "anonymised" patient data to train an AI model, no specific law in his contract mentions this situation. The IEEE/ACM code of ethics gives him a clear rule — avoid harm and respect privacy — so he refuses and reports the offer. If IT staff leaked records, the public would stop trusting the clinic's digital systems.

## 5. Common Ethical Issues for IT Users ⭐

| Issue | 核心点 |
|---|---|
| **Software piracy** | 非法复制、分享、安装软件 |
| **Inappropriate use of computing resources** | 上班时间用公司电脑做私事：购物、玩游戏、看不当内容、做私人生意，浪费公司资源、降低生产力 |
| **Inappropriate sharing of information** | 泄露公司机密或顾客资料（private data 或 confidential business data） |

### ✅ 高分答法 — May 2026 Q1a: *Explain THREE common ethical issues faced by IT users.* (9 marks)

每个 3 分（P-E-S-C）：

1. **Software piracy** – the illegal copying, sharing or installing of software without a valid licence. *Scenario:* to save money, the manager of Kopi Kaki's 8 outlets installs one licensed copy of an accounting package on all 8 branch PCs, although the licence covers only one computer. This deprives the software company of about RM7,000 in licence fees, exposes the PCs to malware hidden in cracked installers, and is an offence under the Copyright Act 1987; the company could face fines and a raid by enforcement officers.
2. **Inappropriate use of computing resources** – using the organisation's computers, network or time for non-work activities. *Scenario:* Daniel, a customer-service officer at KedaiKu, spends about two hours a day watching football highlights and running an online phone-case business on his office PC. Customer complaints wait longer, the office bandwidth slows down for 30 colleagues, and his downloads bring in adware. The company pays for work that is not done and must set an acceptable-use policy and monitoring to control it.
3. **Inappropriate sharing of information** – disclosing confidential company data or customers' private data to people who should not have it. *Scenario:* a MedikaCare nurse forwards a WhatsApp screenshot of a well-known footballer's blood-test result to a friend "just for fun"; within a day it is on social media. This breaches the patient's privacy and the PDPA 2010, damages MedikaCare's reputation, and can lead to her dismissal and a complaint to the Commissioner.

## 6. Certifications in IT

| | **Vendor certification** | **Industry association certification** |
|---|---|---|
| 内容 | 针对**某家公司的产品** | 较广泛，**不绑定单一厂商** |
| 例子 | Cisco (CCNA)、Microsoft、AWS | (ISC)² (CISSP)、ISACA (CISA) |
| 特点 | 考试证明会用该产品；产品改版要重考 | 通常要求工作经验、考试、**持续进修（CPE）** |

**📈 高分情境**
> Nurul has a Cisco CCNA, a vendor certification, so KedaiKu hires her to configure its Cisco switches in the Shah Alam warehouse — it proves she can work with that specific product. Her manager, Farid, holds ISACA's CISA, an industry-association certification that is not tied to any vendor and requires yearly continuing education; it shows he can audit KedaiKu's whole security programme regardless of which brands are used.

## 7. Five Steps of Ethical Decision-Making

```
 ① Develop problem statement → ② Identify alternatives → ③ Evaluate & choose alternative
            ▲                                                         │
            │ (if unsuccessful)                                       ▼
   ⑤ Evaluate the results  ◀────────────  ④ Implement the decision
```

### ✅ 高分答法 — Oct 2024 Q1b: *Describe the FIVE major steps involved in ethical decision-making.* (5 marks)

每步 1 分——**用同一个情境走完五步**（这就是"自己的 scenario"）：

*Scenario:* Aina discovers that her team lead at KedaiKu has been giving his friend a 30% "staff discount" code that is meant only for employees.

1. **Develop a problem statement** – state the issue clearly and gather facts: *"A non-employee has used the staff discount code 42 times in three months, costing KedaiKu about RM3,800."* Aina checks the order logs to confirm the facts before accusing anyone.
2. **Identify alternatives** – list the possible actions: say nothing; speak to the team lead privately; report to the HR manager; post about it in the staff group chat.
3. **Evaluate and choose an alternative** – weigh each option against laws, company policy and its effect on everyone involved. Saying nothing lets the loss continue; posting in the group chat humiliates him; she chooses to speak to him first and report to HR if it continues.
4. **Implement the decision** – carry it out professionally: Aina meets him privately, explains the policy and the RM3,800 loss, and asks him to stop.
5. **Evaluate the results** – check whether the decision solved the problem: a month later the code logs show no further use by the friend. If it had continued, she would return to step 1 and escalate to HR.

## 8. 自己练习的新情境题（附高分答案）

**P1. A school's IT technician can read teachers' emails through the admin console. Using this scenario, explain the difference between what is legal and what is ethical. (4 marks)**
> It may be legal because the school owns the email system and its policy allows administrators access for maintenance. However, if Mr Lim, the technician, reads the principal's private emails about teachers' salaries out of curiosity, it is unethical: professional ethics allow access only when needed for a technical task. He breaks no court-enforced law but violates the trust expected of IT staff and can be disciplined.

**P2. Give an example where an action is ethical but illegal in IT. (3 marks)**
> A security researcher, Hafiz, notices that a government-linked e-voucher website shows other users' IC numbers when he changes one digit in the URL. To prove the danger he downloads 50 records before reporting it. His intention — protecting citizens — may be ethical, but accessing data without authorisation is still an offence under the Computer Crimes Act 1997; he should have reported the flaw without downloading records.

**P3. Explain TWO characteristics of an IT professional with examples. (4 marks)**
> *Commitment to lifelong learning* – Nurul spends weekends studying for her AWS certification because KedaiKu is moving its servers to the cloud next year. *Independent judgment* – when a manager demands that a half-tested payment feature go live before a public holiday sale, Farid uses his own professional judgment to delay it by two days, preventing possible double-charging of customers.

## 9. Cheat sheet

- **Ethics** = shared values of acceptable behaviour · invisible guidebook · computer ethics · culture differs (piracy)。
- **Morals** 个人 · **Ethics** 群体 / 专业 · **Law** 政府；可 legal but unethical，也可 ethical but illegal。
- **Why IT**：governance、work、personal life；balance company interest vs individual rights；privacy、data security、global concerns。
- **IT professional**：advanced knowledge & creativity · independent judgment · lifelong learning · contribution to society；code of ethics（ACM、IEEE）。
- **IT users 3 issues**：software piracy · inappropriate use of resources · inappropriate sharing of information。
- **Certs**：vendor（Cisco、Microsoft、AWS）vs industry association（(ISC)²、ISACA）。
- **5 steps**：problem statement → alternatives → evaluate & choose → implement → evaluate results（不成功回到 ①）。
- **每个点都加**：名字 + 地点 + 数字 + 结果 + 法律。
